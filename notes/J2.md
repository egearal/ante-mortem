# J2 — THE ASCENT v3 (it climbs alone, every rung pays)

## How it feels
- Step on the green gate and press SPACE. You no longer mash: the number **climbs by itself**, and the tick speeds up and rises in pitch as it goes.
  - Reaching 1.77 takes ~2.8s, 2.77 ~5.2s, 3.69 ~6.8s and 7.77 ~11s.
- The ladder names the prize at every rung (I · +1 LEVEL, II · HOT LEVEL, III · THE ARMORY, ☆ ASCENSION). The line under it always says what cashing pays right now and what the next rung pays.
- **SPACE (or ENTER, or the phone coin, which now reads CASH) cashes:**
  - **I:** +1 level at WARM odds + coins.
  - **II:** a HOT ◆◆ level + 25% blood.
  - **III — THE ARMORY:** the letters A·R·M·O·R·Y slam in violet, then a rack of three epic and legendary cards (weapons first, skills fill the rack). Pick one, new at Lv1 or +1 level. Plus coins.
  - **☆ 7.77:** JACKPOT. Pick an Ascension, and when the ride closes **two slot machines open in a row at FEAST odds**.
- Crash first and THE FALL curses you, as before.
- The odds are kinder than before: you now reach the first rung 82% of the time, the second 58%, the third 35% and the summit 12% (was 75/40/15/4).

## What changed
- `ascent.js`:
  - `climbCrash` (per frame, inside `tickCrash`) replaces `pushCrash` and the frenzy. DOM writes happen only on change.
  - The new `CP_ANCH` odds table, and `crash.rigCp`, which survives `openCrash`'s reset (for J3's lesson).
  - New prizes in `cashCrash`; `ascLevel(ft)`.
  - `cardOffer({...})` is now shared by `jackpotOffer` (mint) and the new `armoryOffer` (violet). `pickJackpot` is the generic picker (keys, pad and click unchanged).
  - `closeCrash` uses the `gainXP` level formula.
  - Removed: `pushCrash`, `crashStakeUI`, `prerollAsc`, `przLabel`, `grantAscPassive`, `ASC_POOLS`. `rollAscentPrize` stays because the wheel's ✦ SKILL wedge uses it.
- `ui.js`: SPACE/ENTER both cash while live, key-repeat ignored (a held SPACE can't cash the instant it launches). Coin label is CASH while live. HOW TO PLAY / controls text.
- `styles.css`: on phones the tier slams wrap, and the card rack fits 390px. Cards take the offer's color.
- Probe bot: launches once, cashes at ≥2.9× (`crashAt`), `holdCards` for posed shots.
- `ROADMAP.md` lists "XP/money payouts from the Ascent" as rejected. The director's newer brief (PLAN_FEED, "Ascent rewards change at every tier") overrides it, so the later statement wins.
