# D4 — THE MAJOR ARCANA

Branch `claude/d4-major-arcana`. `dist/` is not committed.

## What it feels like now

Most tarot cards you walk over are still the 24 small fates. Every so often the deck deals from
the **Major Arcana** instead. You know straight away. The House announces
**✦ A MAJOR ARCANUM MANIFESTS ✦** over a low organ swell and a rising chime. A column of gold light
stands on the floor where the card lies. The card is bigger and framed in gilt and violet, and it
slowly turns over to show its glyph. The fate-arrow turns gold. When you come within about
260px, the card's name hangs over it ("XVI · THE TOWER"), so you can choose whether to take it.
Some Majors cost you something.

Picking one up is the loudest thing tarot has ever done:
1. **The toll.** A deep cast bell rings and the room drops into slow motion for about a second. A
   dark veil falls, and gold and violet rays start turning behind a large gilt-and-violet card
   that shows its back (✦).
2. **The flip.** The card turns in 3D. A choir chord climbs eight notes, the bells ring, gold and
   violet flecks burst out, two rings ripple from Vesper, and the name slams across the banner.
3. **The seal.** A seraph shimmer plays. Pacts add a low contract drone and three scratches of a
   pen.

The face of the card shows the roman numeral, the glyph in its own color, the name, a
"MAJOR ARCANA" ribbon, the **full rule with real numbers**, and the duration ("◷ 75s", "⛓ A PACT ·
FOR THE NIGHT", "⚡ AT ONCE"). Timed Majors sit in the HUD buff row with a countdown. When one ends,
"… THE SUN FADES …" plays with a soft three-note sigh.

### The odds (shown in the Index)
Each tarot that manifests is Major on a **5% chance, +1% per HUD minute, capped at 12%**. Only one
Major lies on the floor at a time, and **each arcanum can be drawn once per night**. Tarot comes about
every 14–24s, so a 6-minute night sees about one Major. Long nights see two or three. The chapel's
doubled tarot cadence doubles them too.

### The Index
A new **✦ THE MAJOR ARCANA** section sits under THE TAROT, with gilt tiles in each card's color.
Hovering a tile shows the full rule and its kind. A hint line below it states the odds. The Index
total grew by 12. Majors are catalogued in the existing `IDX.t` array under `MAJOR:<id>` keys, so
no save key changed and no new key was needed.

### Also fixed (in lane)
Many of the 24 small tarot cards' Index one-liners described the wrong effect. For example, THE STAR
said "Heal 40%" but pulls every orb and coin to you, and JUSTICE said "Coins match your kills" but
slays common foes within 320px. Every `dd` string now matches what its `fx` actually does. No
small-card effect or number changed.

## How it's built
- `src/tarot.js`: `MAJOR_ARCANA` table (id, numeral, name, glyph, color, kind, dur, `dd` text,
  ap/on/off). `arcanaRollSpawn` (odds), `arcanaManifest`, `arcanaDraw`, `revealMajor` (DOM ceremony,
  built lazily, so no index.html change), `tickMajors` (all effects, called at the top of `tickTarot`
  so timed arcana keep running while the House walks), `drawMajorCard` and `drawMajorsFX`
  (world-space, called from `drawTarots`). Sounds are synthesized in `ARC_SFX` with
  tone/noise: toll, flip choir, pact drone, fade, zap, beam warn/hit, wheel clack, gavel, tower
  crash, twin shot, sun swell, rise. Run state resets itself when `P` changes (`arcState()`), so
  startGame needed no hook. Timed effects revert through their debuffs entry.
- `GLYPH_COL['ma_<id>']` is registered at boot from tarot.js, so ui.js is untouched.
- Hooks outside tarot.js are one line each:
  - `src/marker.js` `die()`: `arcanaCheatDeath()` (DEATH's skip).
  - `src/slot.js` `weightedByRarity()`: `arcUp()` (the WHEEL's rarity lift).
  - `src/ranks.js`: `idxTotal` + the Index section.
- `styles.css`: one block at the end, `/* ==== D4 ==== */` (#arcanaReveal, with a phone size).
- Debug: `arcanaDraw('tower')` draws one now; `window._arcForce='moon'` makes the next manifesting tarot that Major.

## Verified
- `node probes/arcana.js [--shots]` plays each of the 12 on a fresh night. Every card goes through
  the real path: forced deal → the card lands on the floor → Vesper walks onto it → ceremony →
  effect → expiry. Then it checks the card did what its text says (Tower: coins → 0 and on-screen
  foes dead; Devil: damage ×1.5 and max blood halved; Death: a 99999 hit is skipped at 60% blood;
  Wheel: 0/300 commons dealt; Chariot: speed ×1.30 and then exactly ÷1.3 at expiry; Sun: damage
  taken ×1.25 and then back; plus lightning, beam, wheel, twin and justice counters). It also
  checks that the reveal showed, the Index caught it and the HUD timer appeared, and that there
  were zero console errors.
  **Frame budget:** the arcana's own code (tickMajors + drawMajorsFX + drawMajorCard) is timed on
  the real clock with every frame rendered, p95 ≤ 1.5ms.
  **Result: 12/12 pass, 0 errors.** Arcana code p95 is 0.0–0.2ms per frame. The median
  whole frame is ~2–3ms with or without a Major (headless). Single max outliers (up to ~590ms)
  are the harness's periodic software-GL flush landing inside a draw call; see Known issues.
- Shots are rendered every frame and checked by eye: the reveal sits left of center on desktop
  (clear of the banner lane and of Vesper) and under the banner lane on phones. Sun burn ticks no
  longer flash enemies white, so silhouettes survive high noon.
- Shots in `shots/d4/`: `reveal_tower`, `reveal_death`, `reveal_devil`, `reveal_phone`,
  `ground_major_phone`, `fx_moon`, `fx_chariot`, `fx_star`, `fx_sun`, `fx_lovers`.
- `npm run probe -- --nights 3`: exit 0, zero errors, final run deaths 4:35 / 6:55 / 2:28
  (median 4:34). Noise check with the same bot: unmodified main over 5 nights gave a 4:52 median
  (range 1:57–5:14). D4 over 8 extra nights gave a ~5:30 median (3:06–11:40), and only 2 of those 8
  nights drew a Major, because the bot doesn't seek tarot. Difficulty is not changed
  in any measurable way. Majors are mostly upside, and the costs (Tower, Devil, Sun) are opt-in
  because the card's name shows before you step on it.

## Known issues / notes for the merge
- Headless whole-frame timing on this container shows a periodic ~600ms render stall every ~11th
  frame, even before any arcanum is drawn (software GL flush in the harness). That's why the
  probe budgets the arcana code itself and reports the median frame.
- Timed arcana pause while the slot, wheel or ascent are open, because debuffs only tick in play.
  This is intended.
- THE HANGED MAN and THE SUN multiply `P.dmgTakenMult`. Anyone else who sets it (rather than
  multiplying it) would overwrite them.
- Files touched: `src/tarot.js` (most of it), `src/marker.js` (1 line), `src/slot.js` (1 line),
  `src/ranks.js` (2 spots), `styles.css` (D4 block at end), `probes/arcana.js` (new),
  `shots/d4/` (new), `notes/D4.md`. The only merge risks are those three one-line hooks. Each is
  `typeof`-guarded, so a missing tarot.js can't break them.
