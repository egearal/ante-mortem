# B4 — THE WRECKING CREW

Branch `claude/b4-wrecking-crew`. Quality only: no new features, no restyling. Every fix is a few lines,
tagged `B4` in a comment. Rebased onto main after B1 (daily debt), B2 (stakes + endless), B3 (gamepad + dials),
D1 (the seats) and D3 (the Ballroom) landed.

## How it feels now
The table stops talking over itself. A 10-kill streak gets one voice — the big ⚔ banner — instead of a
small echo stuttering behind it. The COMBO plaque is back where it was always meant to be, centred under
the XP rail, instead of half off the left edge of the screen. On a phone the House's little tips used to
land on top of the gold banners, on the feat toasts and on the ledger's title; now they file into the same
column as everything else and the ledger politely steps down below them. The LEVEL CHARGE gauge used to
hide under Vesper's blood bar on a phone — you literally could not see your XP. Now it rides just above the
blood bar on a phone and beside it on a small laptop or a landscape phone. OPTIONS opens with its title and
QUALITY row visible on a 720p screen (it used to be cut off the top, unreachable). The tarot INDEX finally
tells the truth: fifteen cards were describing a neighbour's effect (JUSTICE said "coins match your kills" and
actually slays the court around you; DEATH said "slay everything near" and actually summons bat-imps).

## Tools added
- **`probes/fuzz.js`** — `node probes/fuzz.js [--events 6000] [--seed 7] [--only desktop|phone|land] [--heap 5]`.
  Random keys (SPACE/ESC/TAB/R/ENTER/F/M/WASD/arrows/1-3/hotkeys, taps, holds, auto-repeat), mouse/touch/pen
  pointers, raw TouchEvents, every visible button, viewport resizes, visibility/blur/focus flips, and churn
  (start, pause, quit, forced deaths, XP/coin grants so the slot/wheel/Ascent open, time skips) at 1280×720,
  390×844 and 844×390. After every 150 events a recovery pass must bring the state machine back to
  playing/menu (a dead table must re-ante), then DOM/state agreement is checked (persistent ghost layers only —
  bigWin/BUST/PERFECT legitimately ride the jackpot layer for ~2s). Arrays bounded; heap + DOM node count
  across 5 consecutive nights in one page. Exit 1 on any failure.
- **`probes/perf.js`** — real-clock `?prof` profile with the court pinned at N enemies around Vesper (god,
  level-ups suppressed). `--matrix` = {300,500} × {desktop q=high, phone 390×844 q=low CPU×4} + extras;
  `--bench` = back-to-back renders of a frozen world for A/B; `--stages N`.

## Known issues (not fixed — out of lane or judged not worth the risk tonight)
- **Phone portrait slot:** the machine fills the screen, so A2's lane logic has no free lane and feat toasts
  (NEW RECORD, BLOODRUSH…) stack over the reels for their ~4s. Needs a design call (hold toasts during a
  phone spin?) — not a bug fix.
- **Phone perf budget** (≤16 ms p95 at q=low, CPU×4) is not met — and was not met before tonight. See Verified.
- On phones the floating BET/AGAIN thumb button sits over the ledger's ANTE UP row while the ledger is
  scrolled to it (same action, but it covers text). A4 territory.
- The HUD stays visible (dimmed) behind the death ledger — pre-existing, A4 noted it.
- Debuff/buff chips carry their own glyph *and* get a ✦/☠ prefix ("✦ ✨ GOLDEN HOUR") — consistent everywhere,
  left alone.
- Coach tips are fixed `z-index:9500` and can float over the OPTIONS panel for their 4.6s life (now dismissable by tap/click).
- Decals age inside `render()` (`dc.t+=1/60`), so their lifetime tracks render rate, not time. Harmless, noted.
- Glyph audit: every codepoint in src/ renders in this container's font set; nothing outside card suits /
  chess / stars / Misc Symbols / Geometric Shapes / common emoji. No alchemical symbols. Nothing changed.
- FAKE-currency audit: no real-money framing anywhere ("no real money — only your soul" on the share card).
