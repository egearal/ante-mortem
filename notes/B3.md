## How it feels
**Pick up a pad and the House notices.** A green slam says *◈ A PAD SITS DOWN AT THE TABLE ◈*. A rising
three-note chime and a chip-stack go with it. A **gilt ring** settles on ♠ PLAY TONIGHT. The ring is a gold
outline that breathes between gold and white-gold, with a velvet gap and a warm halo. A slim brass pill at the
bottom of the screen shows what your hand can do right now: `✚ CHOOSE · Ⓐ SELECT · START PLAY · R-STICK SCROLL`.
Every prompt in the House switches to pad language. "PRESS SPACE" becomes "PRESS Ⓐ", the pause card says
"Ⓨ — YOUR CODEX", WASD becomes L-STICK, and the vault and streak canvas hints say Ⓐ. A PlayStation pad gets
✕ ○ □ △ and OPTIONS. Touch a key or click the mouse and every label turns back into keyboard text. Press a pad
button and they flip again.

The D-pad or stick walks the ring around the menu, and a soft felt tick plays on each step. The ring wraps from the
bottom of a column back to the top. Long panels scroll so the ring stays in view: the Ante Room, Vault, Index,
Feats, Ledger and the death ledger all work this way. Ⓐ chooses, Ⓑ steps back with a falling two-note sigh, and
START at the menu deals the night. In the pit the left stick walks. Ⓐ is the space bar, and holding it rolls the
dice. Ⓧ cashes the Ascent, START pauses and Ⓨ opens the codex. The pad **rumbles** on hits, a heavy thud when
you die, a long roll on a jackpot, a medium thump on a triple lock, a bigWin rumble that grows with the tier, and
a low growl on a boss roar. If the pad is unplugged mid-night, the House pauses and holds your seat.

**THE DIALS** is a brass-and-velvet card that replaces OPTIONS as the first door. Menu OPTIONS opens it, and so
does a new SETTINGS pill on the pause. On phones that button sits on the pause card next to ✦ YOUR CODEX. It has
three sections:
- **♪ SOUND**: MASTER, SOUND EFFECTS and MUSIC sliders. Each one previews as you drag; the SFX slider plays a coin
  ping. LOOK FOR TRACKS is a toggle, and a line underneath tells you which of the five music tracks the House found.
- **✦ FEEL**: SCREEN SHAKE runs 0–150%, and the card itself shudders at the new strength while you drag.
  FLASH INTENSITY previews a gold flash. REDUCE FLASHING caps every flash at a soft glow and kills the Ascent
  strobe, the chroma flicker and the rarity colour-washes on the canvas. PAD RUMBLE is a toggle, and turning it on
  gives the pad a test buzz.
- **⌨ HANDS**: ivory keycaps for MOVE ×4, BET, PAUSE and CODEX. Click one and it pulses gold while it listens.
  Press a key and it's bound with a bright two-note *tick-ting*. If the key is already taken, the two actions
  swap and the card says so ("⇄ SWAPPED — BET ON I · MOVE UP ON SPACE"). ESC keeps the old key. The House keeps
  ESC, F, M, ENTER, R, Q and 1–3 for itself. A rebound key shows as a gold keycap. RESET KEYS deals the House
  keys again.
- **GRAPHICS & MORE ▸** opens the old options card. That card now scrolls, because at 720p its CLOSE button used
  to fall off the bottom of the screen. Closing it brings you back to THE DIALS.

**THE HOUSE BAND**: drop `menu / run / boss / ascent / house` (.ogg/.mp3) into `assets/music/` and rebuild. The
menu then plays its loop, a night crossfades into `run` over 1.6s, a boss swaps in `boss`, the Ascent plays
`ascent`, and THE HOUSE plays `house`. Missing tracks fall back down the chain: house → boss → run, and
ascent → run. Pausing drops the band to 45%, and the codex drops it to 60%. Jackpots, big wins, level-ups, the
vault door, boss roars and your death **duck** it hard and let it breathe back up. The death roll plays to
silence and a heartbeat. The band returns when the ledger has been read. With no files present the game is
exactly as before: silent and error-free.

## What was built (by brief item)
1. **Gamepad** (`src/ui.js`, B3 block at the end)
   - `PAD` + `b3PadFrame()` on rAF. Standard mapping (Xbox / PlayStation / Steam Deck). The first connected pad
     wins. PlayStation is detected by vendor 054c or its name.
   - Pit: core.js `update()` reads `PAD.mx/my` (left stick, deadzone 0.25, D-pad too). Ⓐ sends real
     `keydown`/`keyup` ' ' (the same `tcKey` A4 uses), so hold-to-roll works. Ⓧ = Enter, START = Escape,
     Ⓨ/VIEW = `tcCodex()` (A4's toggle), and Ⓑ in slot/wheel = Escape ("finish the hand").
   - Menus: a **scope** is the topmost visible panel. The list is explicit (`B3_SCOPES`), plus any other `.layer`
     or `*Panel` element, so B1/B2 panels are picked up automatically. If no panel is open, the scope falls back
     to the pause, the ledger, the menu, or the Ascent jackpot cards. Focusables are found generically: buttons,
     inputs, and `B3_CLICKY` cards (soul/floor/contract/indulgence cards, jackpot cards, how-to tabs, loadout line,
     rank bar). A clickable that wraps another clickable is skipped.
   - Navigation is spatial, with row/column gap weighting and wrap-around. Holding a direction repeats after
     360ms, then every 110ms. Scroll-into-view only moves the nearest overflow containers, never the page.
     Focus is remembered per panel. A new pause or ledger always starts on its first choice (RESUME / ANTE UP).
     ◀ ▶ on a slider adjusts it, LB/RB turn pages, and the right stick scrolls.
   - Ⓐ on ANTE UP sends SPACE, so the ledger skip law is unchanged: the first press reads the ledger, the second
     re-antes.
   - Prompts: a reversible MutationObserver relabeler runs only while the pad is the last hand used. A keyboard
     press or mouse click restores the original text. Touch keeps A4's one-way labels. Rebinding keycaps render
     their text via CSS `attr()`, so neither relabeler can rewrite them.
   - Rumble: wrappers on SFX methods → `vibrationActuator.playEffect('dual-rumble')`. It is throttled, a stronger
     effect overrides a weaker one, it only fires while the pad is in use, and the toggle switches it off.
2. **Settings panel**: THE DIALS (`#b3Dials`). New key **`am_dials_v1`** stores `{sfx, music, rumble, calm,
   seek, binds}`. Master, shake and flash stay in `GFX` (`antemortem_gfx_v1`, unchanged) so presets keep
   labelling right. Audio wiring is in `src/sfx.js`: new `sfxBus` and `musicBus` gains sit under `master`, and every
   synth voice (tone/noise/drone/whirr/drumroll/ambience) now connects to `sfxBus`. Rebinding works by
   *translation*: a capture-phase listener turns a trusted press of a bound key into a synthetic press of the key
   the House already listens for. No existing handler changed. Default keys keep working unless another action
   claims them.
   - Also fixed while in the panel: fresh saves showed COLORBLIND PIPS / TAB CODEX = UNDEFINED (A4's known
     issue). They now default to OFF / HOLD.
3. **Music channel** (`src/sfx.js` B3 block, `vite.config.js`, `assets/music/README.md`)
   - Chrome logs `Failed to load resource` as a console error for *any* missing audio file, whether it's an
     `<audio>` element or a fetch, over file:// or http. I tested this. So the game never guesses. The build scans
     `assets/music/`, bakes `window.__AM_MUSIC={ctx:[paths]}` into the HTML, and copies the files to
     `dist/assets/music/`. Vite dev injects the same list. A raw `index.html` or a hand-copied folder can use
     LOOK FOR TRACKS, which is opt-in because it does knock on doors.
   - Over http(s) the band plays through Web Audio (`createMediaElementSource` → `musicBus` → master), so it
     works on iOS, where `element.volume` is ignored. From file://, where the element is opaque to Web Audio, it
     sets `element.volume` instead.
   - Two decks crossfade. Ducking is `musicDuck(ms, depth)`, hooked onto 12 big SFX and `duckDrone`. Nothing plays
     before a user gesture, and every `play()` promise is caught.

    `GAME_DESIGN.md` section, `shots/b3*`.
- **For B1/B2/B4:** a new panel is pad-navigable for free if it's a `.layer` or `#…Panel` and its clickables are
  `<button>`s. Clickable `<div>`s need a selector added to `B3_CLICKY` (ui.js). Panels that should beat others go
  in `B3_SCOPES` (ordered topmost-first). New prompt text containing SPACE / ENTER cash / ESC / TAB gets pad
  glyphs automatically.
- **Risky spots**
  - The capture-phase keydown on window runs before every other key handler. It only acts on **trusted** events
    (the bot and tcKey dispatch untrusted ones, so they pass straight through). It also eats ESC and SPACE while
    THE DIALS is open.
  - `flash` is wrapped (reduce-flashing), and so are several `SFX.*` methods (rumble + duck). Anyone who wraps
    them again should keep calling through.
  - Music only plays files listed in `window.__AM_MUSIC`, which is injected just before the main inline script
    in dist. If someone changes the single-file plugin, keep `musicTag(...)` in front of the inline script.

## Known issues
- Pad rebinding isn't offered: the pad layout is fixed, as the brief asked. Pressing a pad button while a keycap
  is listening cancels the capture with a note.
- In headless probes the menu's PLAY button fades in on real CSS time. On a cold boot the ring can land on THE
  INDEX for a moment, then hops to PLAY once it arrives (only if you haven't moved yet).
- LOOK FOR TRACKS (opt-in) prints a harmless "not found" line in the browser log for each absent file. That's why
  it's off by default.
- The Ⓐ Ⓑ Ⓧ Ⓨ glyphs are enclosed alphanumerics. They render on Windows, macOS, Linux/Steam Deck (DejaVu/Noto)
  and Android. PlayStation pads use ✕ ○ □ △.
