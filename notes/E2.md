# E2 — THE HOUSE'S BOOK OF DEBTS

Branch `claude/e2-book-of-debts`. origin/main (B4 + B1/B2 final touches) is merged in. No dist is committed.

## What it feels like now
**The House keeps a ledger on you, and now you can read it.** A new door now stands first in the hall's column:
**⚖ THE BOOK OF DEBTS**. Under its name a small line always says what you're closest to ("NEAREST: THE MAGPIE · 84%").
When something has been unsealed since your last visit, a red **wax seal** sits on the door. It reads NEW! or "3 NEW!", it
breathes, and the first time the hall sees it a low wax-stamp thud plays.

Open it and a leather-and-vellum thump plays, the pages riffle and a soft C-major chord rises. You get a bronze-bound
book in A2's GILT NOIR look: the struck-gold title "THE BOOK OF DEBTS · WHAT THE HOUSE STILL OWES YOU", a total ("6 / 112
SEALS BROKEN") with a gold bar, and a row of chapter tabs.

At the top are **TONIGHT'S DEBTS**: three brass-framed cards, each numbered I, II, III in a blood-red wax seal. They are
the three things you are nearest to, taken from different chapters. Each one shows a big shimmering progress bar with the
percent and the exact count, for example "THE MAGPIE — 84% · 4,212 / 5,000 COINS", "THE DROUGHT — 21 / 25 DEATHS" and
"RANK 15 · GAMBLER V — 9,000 / 10,818 MARKS". This is the one-more-night screen.

Below that are nine **illuminated chapters**. Each opens with a gilt drop-capital in a brass box, like a manuscript:
- **SOULS**: every seat with its D1 portrait. Unsealed seats show in candlelight; sealed seats show the black
  silhouette with burning eyes, the exact condition and a progress bar.
- **FLOORS**
- **STAKES**: "BEAT THE NIGHT · 8 / 15 MINUTES", then a soul × deck grid. Each column is a deck with its glyph, its
  multiplier and its first-clear reward. Each cell shows ◆ cleared, ◇ open, or · sealed.
- **PAIRINGS**: unknown recipes read "??? + ???". Once that weapon is maxed, C1's whispered hint appears ("ONE HALF
  KNOWN"). A discovered pairing shows the full recipe.
- **MAJOR ARCANA**: with the real odds.
- **BOSSES**: "??? — A BOSS UNMET", then "MET · NOT YET SLAIN", then "PAID · SLAIN 2×".
- **CONTRACTS**: plus the bought contract slots.
- **RANK REWARDS**: every 5th rank's forever-perk, counted in marks.
- **DAILY STREAK**: rungs 1/2/3/5/7/11, each with the gilt it adds.

Paid entries glow in their own color with ✦ PAID. Sealed ones sit dashed with a small wax dot. Anything paid since your
last visit gets a red **NEW!** stamp that slams down and glows, and the book plays a paid fanfare: a stamp, a rising
five-note arpeggio, bells, confetti and a little coin rain, with the banner "✦ 3 DEBTS WERE PAID SINCE YOUR LAST VISIT ✦".

**The death ledger** gains one line under the rank bar: **✒ NEAREST DEBT — THE WIDOW · 4 / 12 WHISPERS**. Its bar starts
where the night began. As the tally lands it climbs, the number counts up, and a rising tick plays at each tenth. Then
"+33% TONIGHT" lights in mint. If the night actually paid a debt, the line becomes a stamp instead:
**✦ DEBT PAID ✦ THE MAGPIE**, with the paid fanfare, a flash in that debt's color and confetti over the ledger.
Rank rewards are left out of this line because the rank bar right above it already shows them.

## What was built (by brief item)
1. **The door + the book** (`src/ui.js`, E2 block at the end; `styles.css` one `/* ==== E2 ==== */` block at the end).
   - **Nothing is typed twice.** `e2Chapters()` reads the real tables live:
     - SOULS + D1's `SOUL_PROG`
     - FLOORS
     - STAKES / `STK` / `b2Avail` / `b2BestFor`
     - `PAIRINGS` + `IDX.pr` / `IDX.pm` (C1)
     - `MAJOR_ARCANA` + `ARC_ODDS` + `IDX.t 'MAJOR:id'` (D4)
     - BOSS_DEFS + `LT.bossBy` + `IDX.b`
     - RUN_CONTRACTS + CT_SLOT_COST
     - RANKS + `rkCum`
     - `DLY` / `dlyStreak()`

     Gates that exist only as text (`uTxt`: "5,000 LIFETIME KILLS", "SURVIVE 10:00 (OR CLEAR STAKE 1)", "MEET THE HOUSE
     3 TIMES"…) are parsed by `E2_RX` for their target number. OR-alternatives take the best one. Whether an entry is
     sealed or paid always comes from the table's own `u()`.
   - Exceptions:
     - The 15:00 night length is a named constant `E2_NIGHT_MIN`.
     - The daily-streak gilt mirrors daily.js' inline formula (5 × (streak−1), capped at 11 days). There is no table
       to read for it.
   - Hotkey **B** opens the book from the hall. ESC closes it. SPACE is swallowed while it's open, so it never deals a
     night from under the book. Clicking an entry gives a little poke animation and a tick.
2. **Three marked debts** (`e2Pins()`):
   - They are the highest-% unpaid entries, one per chapter first.
   - Purchases (contract slots) only count once you hold 25% of the price. The third slot only counts after the second.
   - Pure-luck or binary entries (stake decks, arcana, the House boss) never pin.
   - Rank gates are measured in **marks**, not rank number. "Rank 1 of 12" is not 8% of the way.
   - On a fresh save, where nothing is measurable yet, it falls back to RANK 5 → THE DEALER → THE CELLAR.
   - **Ledger:** every entry is snapshotted when a night begins. It watches `_runSeq`, because `startGame` is bound by
     reference in places and can't be wrapped. After `actuallyDie` the line is written into `#goRank`.
   - **NEW! seal:** it shows paid entries that are not yet in `E2.seen`.
3. **Phone / pad / touch.**
   - On phones the book fills the glass with safe-area insets. The chapter tabs scroll sideways, the chapters scroll with
     touch, and everything collapses to one column. The door spans both columns of the phone menu grid.
   - Pad: `#e2Book` is registered in B3's `B3_SCOPES`, with default focus `.e2Pin`. Every entry and stake row is a
     `<button>`, so B3's ring walks it and scrolls it into view. Ⓑ closes it.

**Persistence:** new additive key **`am_book_v1`** `{v:1, seen:[paid entry ids], dsMax}`. The first boot seeds `seen`
with everything already paid, so an old save doesn't light up with 40 NEW! stamps. Every other key is read-only.

## Known issues
- A sealed soul's progress follows D1's `SOUL_PROG` (rank-gated Dealer is measured in marks for pinning, shown as "RANK 1 / 12").
- A new unlock condition whose wording matches no `E2_RX` row still shows correctly as sealed or paid, but its bar
  reads "0 / 1". Add one regex row to `E2_RX` (ui.js) for any new phrase.
- Daily streak "paid" rungs use the best streak the book has *seen* (`dsMax`). B1 only stores the current streak, so
  a streak earned before E2 existed counts only from its current value.
- C2 (bosses) isn't merged here. The BOSSES chapter reads BOSS_DEFS generically, so new bosses appear with their `g`
  glyph when they land, and THE HOUSE (housegod) is shown as "OUTLAST ITS REIGN — STAND AT 15:00".
