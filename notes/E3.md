# E3 — THE SOUND OF THE HOUSE (audio pass)

Branch `claude/e3-sound`, built from origin/main @ 9f15c58 (Wave A + B3 + D1 + D3 merged).

## How it sounds now
**The whole House plays in one key: D.** It has a four-note signature: up a fifth, a step down, then home an
octave higher. *Ta-ta-ta—DAAH.* You hear it everywhere money or fate turns:
- **The level-up slot** plays it fast on a music box over a warm bass, and a shimmer of high chimes follows.
- **A jackpot** (the golden chip 777, and bigWin tier 3+) rings it on bells an octave up. There's a boom under it, a
  bright chord on the last note, and a short sparkle of coins after.
- **A rank-up** on the death ledger plays it slow and churchy on an organ, over the seraph choir. The bells come
  in a beat later.
- **THE HOUSE arriving** at 13:00 gets the sub-drop, then the same motif *bent wrong*. It's low, on reed-saw
  and sine, slow. The third note slips a semitone and the last falls *below* home instead of rising. A far bell
  tolls three times. You know the tune by then, so hearing it break is the scary part.

**Near miss** now holds its breath. A trembling minor-second chord swells for ~0.6s (the "ONE ROW OFF" beat),
then sighs down two notes. **A triple** gets the full cascade. The machine inhales (a rising swell while the reels
hold), then a boom, the motif up high, and a waterfall of fourteen bells tumbling down the pentatonic to the floor.
The existing triple ceremony (bigWin, bells, seraph) lands on top of it.

**The pickup scales are tuned to the House key.** Coins climb a D-major scale starting on A, bright, and step up
an octave after seven. XP gems climb D pentatonic, softer and lower. The two never clash, even when you vacuum a
whole screen at once. Kill ticks climb pentatonic too.

**Storms don't turn to noise.** Kills that land faster than 32ms apart merge into the next one. That one plays
with a little crunch whose size matches how many it swallowed. A 300-kill nova storm reads as dense, crunchy
chatter instead of white noise. Hits, shots, crits, coins and XP have per-sound repeat windows. Each category also
gets quieter per voice once it gets crowded (a density knee).

**Every floor has its own room**, very low under the game:
- **The Pit**: a crowd murmur in two formant bands, each breathing on its own slow swell. Chips clack at far
  tables. Every 14–26s a roulette a few tables over plays: the ball skitters, slows, drops, and sometimes the far
  table gasps.
- **The Cellar**: low stone rumble. Water drips into its own echo, panned around you. The swarm skitters in the
  walls in quick little grain-bursts.
- **The Chapel**: an organ that never stops praying. Dm → B♭ → Gm → A, each chord gliding into the next with no
  clicks, a slow filter breathing on it, and a faint choir breath above.
- **The Ballroom**: a softer crowd and the odd clink of crystal in the mirrors. D3's music-box waltz is now
  part of this bed, so it ducks with the room.

Every big moment **ducks the room**: jackpot, triple, level-up, rank-up, the House, boss roars, vault, death,
near miss. The bed drops ~16 dB and breathes back up over ~0.3s. The melee (shots, kills, pickups) drops by about
half, so the sting sings. **The director's music still wins.** While a B3 track is playing, the floor beds sink to a
third of their level.

**Nothing clips.** The master chain is now a glue compressor, then a brick limiter, then a soft-ceiling shaper that
mathematically never reaches full scale.

## What was built
1. **Mixer map** (`src/sfx.js`, "E3 — THE MIXING DESK" + "E3 — THE WIRING"). There are nine category buses:
   `ui, combat, kill, pickup, slot, jackpot, boss, voice, amb`. Each has a trim (`E3_MIX`), a voice cap (`E3_CAP`)
   and a density knee (`E3_KNEE`). Routing is: bus → B3 `sfxBus` (SFX slider) → `master` (MASTER slider) →
   comp → limiter → ceiling. `musicBus` (MUSIC slider) is unchanged. `e3Wire()` runs on `load` and at
   `initAudio`. It wraps every `SFX.*` method, including the ones ui.js/slot.js/D1 add, files it under its
   category (`E3_CAT`), applies its repeat window (`E3_GAP`) and duck (`E3_DUCK`). It also wraps D3's `BAL_SFX`
   (→ voice) and `balMusic` (→ amb). Delayed notes keep their category: inside a wrapped call `setTimeout` is
   briefly wrapped so the callbacks inherit it. Direct `tone()`/`noise()` calls that aren't inside an SFX method
   get a category from the game state: slot/wheel/crash/vault/marker → slot, menu/paused/dead → ui, else combat.
   - `tone()`/`noise()` gained an optional trailing `delay` argument, which schedules on the audio clock and
     stays backwards compatible. `noise()` now reuses one shared looping noise buffer instead of allocating a new
     buffer per call (a perf win in storms). The old "14 voices, soft ones yield" pool is replaced by the
     per-bus caps (global safety 140/190).
2. **Signature pass**: `houseMotif(root, opts)` plus `SFX.levelUp / jackpot / bigWin / level` rewritten around it.
   New `SFX.triple`, `SFX.rankUp`, `SFX.houseArrive`, `SFX.nearMiss` (tense hold via `e3Hold`). There are also new
   hot-path methods: `SFX.kill`, `SFX.streakStep`, `SFX.coinStep`, `SFX.xpStep`, `SFX.crit`.
3. **Beds**: `e3Bed(floor)` is called by `startAmbience()`, which reads `curFloor()`. Its loop noise is
   crossfaded into itself so there's no seam. Events are scheduled 0.4s ahead on the audio clock. If a new night
   starts on a different floor, the old bed is swapped out. The GFX ambience toggle still turns it off.
4. **Master limiter**: in `initAudio`.

## Call-site changes outside sfx.js (one line each)
- `src/core.js`: the kill tones → `SFX.kill(killStreak)`. The streak scale tone → `SFX.streakStep`. The coin
  combo tone → `SFX.coinStep(P._coinCombo)`. The XP combo tone → `SFX.xpStep(P._xpCombo)`. The unthrottled
  per-bullet crit tone → `SFX.crit()`. At the House arrival, `tone(38…)` → `SFX.houseArrive()`.
- `src/marker.js`: rank-up `SFX.seraph(); SFX.dingBells();` → `SFX.rankUp()`, which still plays both.
- `src/slot.js`: the triple-hold moment adds `SFX.triple()`.
- `package.json`: `npm run audio`.

## Verified (how)
- `npm run build` is clean.
- **`npm run audio`** (new `probes/audio.js`) swaps the page's AudioContext for an **OfflineAudioContext**. It
  drives timers and `performance.now` from the offline clock and renders scripted sequences through the real
  graph, with a fresh page per sequence. It reports peak, loudest-100ms RMS, mean RMS and clipped samples. It
  fails if the after-build clips a single sample or logs an error. `--before FILE` compares against an older build
  (I used `git show origin/main:dist/ante-mortem.html`). `--wav DIR` writes the renders so you can listen to them.
  Results (dBFS; full table in `probes/audio-report.json`):

  | sequence | before peak / mean | after peak / mean | clips b→a |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | combat (60 shots+180 hits/s) | −6.2 / −22.2 | −11.9 / −29.0 | 0→0 |
  | 300-kill storm | −6.7 / −24.4 | −14.7 / −32.7 | 0→0 |
  | kills + streak milestones | −2.0 / −15.2 | −1.2 / −13.7 | 0→0 |
  | coin+XP vacuum | −5.9 / −22.5 | −7.3 / −25.1 | 0→0 |
  | slot pull + near miss | −3.2 / −22.8 | −1.6 / −22.2 | 0→0 |
  | triple ceremony stacked | −2.1 / −19.1 | −1.2 / −17.4 | 0→0 |
  | level-up + rank-up | −2.8 / −19.9 | −1.7 / −18.2 | 0→0 |
  | HOUSE arrival + roar + death | −6.6 / −17.7 | −3.7 / −17.0 | 0→0 |

  The room-bed bus ducks −1.9 → −18.4 dB under a jackpot and is back to −2.1 dB 2.9s later.
  Honest note: the old chain did not hard-clip in these scenes. Its −18 dB compressor squashed everything
  instead, and chaos rode at −0.5 dBFS. The new chain gives the melee ~6–8 dB less (it no longer masks the
  stings), lets the ceremonies stand ~1–2 dB prouder, and has a ceiling that cannot be crossed.
- `npm run probe -- --nights 3`: exit 0, 0 errors, two runs: 5:53/4:38/4:15 (median 4:37) and 8:24/6:02/3:48
  (median 6:02), level 4–6. These are within the bot's normal noise, and no difficulty number was touched.
  Floors: cellar 3:15, ballroom 2:31, chapel 4:39 / 6:43 / 4:38, all with 0 errors. The pre-E3 build also died at
  2:10 on the chapel. `--god --minutes 14` reached 14:11 through the House arrival with 0 errors.
- `npm run music` 13/13 and `npm run pad` 39/39, 0 errors. B3's ducks and rumble wrappers still fire, because my
  wrapper calls through them.

## Known issues
- One early chapel night ended "alive at 2:25" with no stall flagged. Three reruns were normal, and the bot's state
  counts showed nothing odd. I believe it was a long ceremony/gate wait, not audio (audio can't touch game logic).
- I can't listen in a container. All tuning is by meter plus reasoning. `node probes/audio.js --wav out/` renders
  everything to .wav for a real ear.
- Ambience levels are deliberately very low (−43 to −49 dBFS mean). If the director wants more room, raise
  `E3_MIX.amb`.

## Merge notes
- Files: `src/sfx.js` (most of it), one-liners in `src/core.js`, `src/marker.js`, `src/slot.js`, `package.json`,
  new `probes/audio.js`, `probes/audio-report.json`, this note. No CSS, no save keys, no new visible glyphs.
- **Anyone adding a new SFX method**: it's picked up automatically on `load`. Add its name to `E3_CAT` to file it
  under the right bus (unfiled methods inherit the caller's/state category), and to `E3_GAP` if it can machine-gun.
- **Risky spot**: `_e3In` swaps `window.setTimeout` for the *synchronous* duration of an SFX call and always
  restores it in `finally`. Code that captures `setTimeout` by reference during an SFX call would keep the
  wrapper, which is harmless because it calls through.
- If another branch also edits `initAudio`, `tone`, `noise` or `startAmbience`, take E3's versions and re-apply
  their delta. B3's `_sfxOut()` routing is preserved underneath the buses.
