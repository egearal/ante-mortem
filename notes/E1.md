# E1 — THE COURT IN MOTION (enemy and projectile art, pass two)

Branch `claude/e1-court-motion`. `dist/` not committed. Zero gameplay numbers changed: no hitbox,
HP, speed, damage, timer or spawn value is touched. Bosses' draw code is untouched.

## What it feels like now
**The court has faces.** Every regular enemy is now its own creature, readable by silhouette alone at
storm density. Each one turns to face Vesper and walks in a 4-frame procedural cycle:
- **Shambler, the Lost Gambler.** A hunched corpse in a rotted dinner jacket with a blood-red tie.
  One arm reaches forward, still holding its last playing card. It lurches and lists, head lolling,
  one red eye.
- **Husk, the Ember Husk.** A charred sprinter leaning into a full run, with live-coal cracks down its
  ribs and ember eyes. It sheds sparks as it goes.
- **Swarm, the bat-imp.** Wings with bone fingers beat in four frames (up, glide, down, glide), with a whip tail and red eyes.
- **Bomber, the Powder Keg.** An iron bomb on stubby legs with a skull grin painted on and an orange
  band. It waddles. **Telegraph:** the fuse always burns and spits sparks. When she steps inside its
  74px blast radius, the radius appears on the floor as a pulsing orange dashed ring, and the keg
  starts throbbing heat-orange.
- **Leech.** A three-ringed slug that bunches and stretches as it crawls. It has a lamprey ring of
  bone teeth, glowing poison glands and feeding tendrils. Just before it heals the court, its glands
  flare green.
- **Shooter, the skeletal archer.** A bone-ash hood with ribs showing through the rags and a quiver of
  amber arrows. The bone bow tracks her. **Telegraph:** during the wind-up the string visibly draws
  back and the arrowhead kindles before the shot.
- **Wraith, the banshee.** A legless shroud with hair streaming behind it, arms already reaching and claws
  out. A hollow skull with ice-blue pupils. It floats, bobs and is slightly translucent.
- **Stalker, the spider-blade.** Seen from above: four steel-tipped legs a side in an alternating gait,
  a chevroned abdomen and a cluster of yellow eyes.
- **Hexer, the warlock.** A needle-tall hood with acid runes stitched down the robe and a face that is
  only one green eye. Its hands are raised. **Telegraph:** a rune circle turns on the floor under it,
  and the orb swells between its hands as the cast charges.
- **Brute, the chained ogre.** A humped wall of red meat with a sunk tusked head, anvil fists and a
  broken chain. It lumbers, squashing on every footfall.
- **Revenant, the dread knight.** Crimson plate, a sword over the shoulder, a tower shield marked with
  a bone cross and a visor slit of embers. It takes heavy, squashing steps.
- **Elite, the Marquis.** A porcelain mask and a violet cape spread like a bat's with a fluttering hem,
  under a collar of knives. It keeps the violet aura, gold crown, motes and purse-bar.
- **Gilded soul.** A gold-leafed gambler sprinting off with the House's coin sack over its shoulder. A
  shimmer sweeps across it.

Visual sizes grew 1.1–1.3× where it helps reading. **Hitboxes are unchanged.** Every body keeps
A1's language: an ink cut-out plate, the white hit-flash in its own shape, and eyes that glow and
drift toward her. The rim light is now true silhouette rim light: a warm copy of the body, nudged
toward her candle, peeks out on the side facing her. The far court (LOD) draws the very same
animated frames, so distant enemies look like small copies of near ones instead of generic blobs.

**They die by family.** The existing gore ledger, blood and A1 tier pops are still there; on top of that:
- **Flesh BURSTS** (shambler, swarm, stalker, leech, brute). The body swells white-hot and pops, flinging meat
  chunks. You hear a wet, low thud.
- **Bone and plate CRUMBLE** (archer, husk, revenant, gilded). The body breaks into three slabs that
  slump, tilt and fall away while darkening to ash. Bone chips scatter (embers for the husk,
  gold for the gilded). You hear a dry two-tick rattle.
- **Spirits DISSOLVE** (wraith, hexer, Marquis). The body rises, thins and stretches into violet smoke
  puffs. You hear an airy falling whisper.
- **The keg goes up.** A fireball the size of its real blast radius, a smoke ring, and the shell
  flying apart. You hear a sub-boom under the existing blast.

**The arsenal stopped being dots.** Eight weapons used to fire the same red-orange dot:
- **Bone Bolt** now throws ivory bone shards with a hot trail.
- **Cylinder** fires brass slugs.
- **Coffin Nails** are iron nails with a rust trail.
- **Dead Lantern** shoots cyan will-o-wisps.
- **Rat King's Fangs** release ghost rat-skulls that snap their jaws as they hunt.
- **Grave Moths** are fluttering two-frame moths.
- **Loaded Die** is a tumbling ivory die cycling through its faces in a violet glow.
- **Chip Mortar** lobs a spinning house chip in a high arc while its shadow runs along the floor.
- **Card Shark** is now a fan of five spectral cards sweeping across the throw.

Weapons that already had a shape got more:
- **Dealer's Hand** cards flip end over end. The face shows a suit (♠♦♥♣ with an A), the back shows
  the House's crimson and gold pattern, a glint catches at the turn, and two afterimages trail.
- **Fan of Knives** blades spin end over end with a two-ghost motion smear.
- **Reaper's Scythe** leaves a crescent smear fading behind the blade.
- **Funeral Bell** now rings visibly: three staggered gold shock rings and a bronze bell swinging
  over her, with a low gong partial under the existing chime.
- **Poison Chalice** pools bubble, and the bubbles rise and pop in rings.
- **The Guillotine** raises a scaffold (two posts, a beam, the blade hauled high) around its shadow
  before it falls. The slam throws a dust cloud, leaves a crack in the marble for three seconds and
  adds a gravel crunch.
- **Gramophone** waves carry ♪ ♫ notes.
- **Roulette Orbit** chips drag a red smear.
- **Blood Nova** flashes a blood disc.

**Every crit** of any weapon now carries a rotating gold four-point glint at its head and a gold
trail. LUCKY shots get a bigger one.

## Evolved weapons (C1)
C1 (`claude/c1-pairings`) was **not merged** when I started. I checked its branch: the evolutions
draw themselves in their own `drawPairings()` from their own arrays (`evoPlants` and so on), not through
`bullets`. So nothing in E1 conflicts with it, and its evolved art stays C1's. The normal bullets
those evolutions keep firing (e.g. Ossuary's bolts) get E1's shard look and the crit glint for free.

## Perf (the Batch 16 discipline)
Pinned 300-enemy storm at 5:00 (every regular type plus elites), 1280×720, q=high, every frame
rendered, real clock around `render()` and around every `drawEnemy` call, 400 frames. This
container rasterizes on a software GPU whose ~300ms flushes land every few frames whatever is on screen,
so, as in B2's gate, those flush frames are split out and the rest are the frames' CPU work. A/B builds
are swapped into `dist/` alternately (main built from origin/main in a worktree).

| 300 storm, q=high | main | E1 |
|---|---|---|
| render CPU p50 / **p95** | 3.4 / **5.8–5.9 ms** | 3.0–3.1 / **5.4–5.8 ms** |
| drawEnemy loop CPU p50 / **p95** | 1.8 / **3.3–3.4 ms** | 1.4–1.6 / **2.6–3.1 ms** |

(Three E1 runs, two main runs. The last E1 run, after caching each enemy's sprite set on the enemy:
render p95 5.4 ms, drawEnemy p95 2.6 ms.)

The court is **cheaper** than it was, despite more detail. The old near court drew 20–60 path fills
and strokes per enemy per frame; the new one draws 4–6 `drawImage` calls. Discipline kept:
- Every body, ink plate, rim, wound, heat, glow and white-flash variant is baked once per
  (type, frame, palette). Blur exists only at bake time.
- Zero per-frame `shadowBlur`, zero per-frame gradient creation.
- Each enemy caches its sprite set, so a frame's lookups are array reads, not string keys.
- qLow skips the rim copy.
- GFX.lite skips eyes, motes and the bomber's spark particles, and caps corpses at 10 (44 otherwise).
- In a storm (`_dense`), dashes and flourish particles yield.

Bake memory: 13 types × 4 frames × ~4 modes ≈ 200 canvases of 112–168 px (~8–12 MB), lazily, only
for types actually seen. `_CT` evicts its oldest quarter past 520 entries.

## Files touched (merge notes)
- `src/fx.js`: one appended block, `/* ==== E1 — THE COURT IN MOTION ==== */`:
  - `_courtArt` (13 designs on a small R-unit path DSL), `courtBake`, `courtSet`, `courtDraw`,
    `_courtCaster`, `_courtCrown`
  - corpses: `courtDeath`, `drawCorpses`
  - arsenal: `_bvk`, `arsenalTrail`, `arsenalDraw`, `arsenalGround`, `arsenalOver`, sprite bakers
  - plus **one added line** at the end of A1's `killFX`: `courtDeath(e)`
- `src/core.js`: six one-line hooks, no other change:
  - `drawEnemy` first line: `if(!e.boss && courtDraw(e)) return;`. Bosses, and any enemy type the
    court doesn't know (e.g. a new C2 type), fall through to the old painter unchanged.
  - after the enemy loop in `render`: `drawCorpses();`
  - after the guillotine block (ground effects): `arsenalGround();`
  - the bullet trail loop: `if(arsenalTrail(b)) continue;`
  - the bullet body loop: `if(arsenalDraw(b)) continue;`
  - after the bullet loop: `arsenalOver();`
- `probes/e1shots.js`, `probes/e1sheet.js` (new); `shots/e1-before/`, `shots/e1-after/`, `shots/e1-phone/`.
- No changes to weapons.js, styles.css, UI/HUD, save keys, CFG numbers, or boss code.

Risky spots:
- C1 adds `drawPairings()` right before `drawPlayer()` and `tickPairings` in update. My hooks are
  elsewhere, so there should be no textual conflict.
- C2 (new bosses) edits boss draw code inside `drawEnemy`, below my first-line hook. Bosses never
  take my path, so C2's bosses keep their own painter. C2 also draws elite affixes
  (`hrDrawAffix(e,r)` inside the old elite block). Elites *do* take my path, so `courtDraw`
  already calls `hrDrawAffix(e,e.r)` itself, guarded by `typeof`: LOADED/MARKED badges appear
  as soon as C2 merges, and the call is a no-op until then. On merge, keep C2's line in the old
  block too (it still serves any fallback type).
- B2's `b2DrawAdorn` (endless champions) draws on its own, untouched.
- The weapon visual kind is inferred from the fields each `fire*()` already sets (`blast` → mortar,
  `bounce` → die, `seek`+r → fang/moth, r≥19+pierce → phantom, no `crit` → nail/wisp, no `pierce`
  → cylinder round), cached per bullet as `b._vk`. If a later change alters those fields, the bullet
  simply falls back to the bone-shard look. Nothing breaks.
