# H1 — THE LAUNCH KIT

Branch `claude/h1-launch-kit`. Everything lives in `launch/` (see `launch/README.md`). No gameplay code changed.

## What
- **Covers and capsules** painted on a canvas in the GILT NOIR style, at exact store sizes:
  itch 630×500 and 315×250, Steam header 460×215, Steam main 616×353, and a 1920×1080 hero.
  Each is a real Ballroom storm frame (a "clean plate" with the HUD lifted, softly blurred and graded down into noir).
  On top sit a velvet vignette, a gilt double frame with ruby-pinned corner diamonds, Vesper's cameo and the
  ANTE / MORTEM lockup in gold leaf with a ♠ rule and THE HOUSE ALWAYS BLEEDS.
  - The cameo is the same paint code as the Ante Room, re-rendered at 10×.
  - The lockup uses the game's own embedded Cinzel and Cormorant Garamond.
- **8 screenshots at 1920×1080** (`launch/screenshots/`):
  1. 300 of the damned on the Ballroom floor.
  2. The brass slot mid-triple: two reels matched, the last one creeping in.
  3. RADIANT — MAX LEVEL under a coin storm.
  4. The Pairing: THE EYE IN THE SKY.
  5. The Croupier calling his coin.
  6. The Tower's Major Arcanum.
  7. The Book of Debts on a mid-progress save.
  8. A phone plate: the real 1170×2532 portrait night in a gilt handset, captioned IN YOUR POCKET.
- **`gameplay-loop.gif`**: 13s, 480×270, 7.6MB, seamless, made by cross-fading its last second into its first.
  There is also a sharp `gameplay-loop.webm` (1280×720, 4MB).
  Both were recorded frame by frame on the virtual clock, so the clip never stutters even though headless rendering is slow.
- **`social-1200x630.png`**, wired into `index.html` as `og:image` and `twitter:image` with a `summary_large_image` card.
  The URL is absolute, `https://egearal.github.io/ante-mortem/launch/social-1200x630.png`, because scrapers ignore relative URLs.
  The card's copy says "FAKE CURRENCY ONLY".
- **`STORE_PAGE.md`** contains:
  - The itch pitch, feature bullets, controls table, tags and fake-currency line.
  - A Steam short description (271 chars) and a long description in Steam BBCode.
  - Steam tags and an honest "simulated gambling: yes" content-survey note.
  - A 280-char social post in the House's voice.
- **`launch/itch/` + `ante-mortem-itch.zip`**: `index.html` (the built single file), manifest, icons and the offline worker, all at the zip root.
  `itch/README.md` gives the upload settings: kind HTML, 1280×720 viewport, mobile friendly, fullscreen button on, autostart off, no scrollbars.
- **Tools**: `npm run launch:shots | launch:compose | launch:gif | launch:itch`.
  The harness (`probes/lib.js`) gained two opt-in `launch()` options: `scale` (device pixel ratio) and `seed` (seeded `Math.random`).

## How it feels
The capsule reads at thumbnail size: a gold-leaf title and a candle-lit cameo against a dark crimson felt seething with bodies.
The screenshots pick the dopamine beats: the reel that hasn't landed yet, the word MAX blazing over falling coins, a ceremony card, a boss table.
The GIF is pure storm: blades orbiting, a chandelier crashing, the FEVER flare.

## Verification
- `npm run build` clean · `npm run probe -- --nights 3` exit 0 · `node probes/daily-check.js` exit 0 — all three passed on the final build.
- Every capture and compose run reported zero page errors.
- The itch `index.html` boots from file://, takes SPACE and plays with zero console errors.
- I viewed every output image. Sizes checked: the GIF is under 8MB and every cover matches its store size exactly.

## Leftovers
- **Seen while capturing (a real UI overlap, not fixed — no gameplay/UI changes in this brief):** at 1280×720, the boss
  health-bar title (THE CROUPIER — 100% · ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣ odds) sits in the same strip as the ×COMBO / STREAK label, so they
  overprint each other during boss fights. Worth a lane in the A2 notice system.
- og:image only resolves once `launch/` is on `main` (GitHub Pages serves main's root).
- Steam asks for more art than the brief listed. These still need making: small capsule 231×87, vertical 374×448,
  library capsule 600×900, library hero 3840×1240 and a transparent logo. `compose.js` layouts can be extended for them.
- The cameo is flat vector art. At hero size its rim light looks graphic rather than painted. Commissioned key art would beat it on a real Steam page.
- The GIF is 480 wide because a 200-body storm is GIF's worst case. Post the WebM wherever video is accepted.
