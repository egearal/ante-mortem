# G1 — LATE-NIGHT POLISH

Branch `claude/g1-polish` from origin/main @ 1d8d739. `dist/` not committed.

## What changed

### 1. The death ledger fits a 720p laptop (F1 known issue 1)
Below 820px of screen height, on screens wider than a phone, the ledger switches to a **compact layout**. It's the same brass
page with A2's look, only tighter:
- The soul's portrait (now 50×60) sits **beside** its rows (name · floor, wave/curses/scars/dice/best pull, chips), so it no longer stacks above them.
- **SHARE YOUR NIGHT · THE BOOKS · THE MENU** share one line of three under ANTE UP.
- The "Soon: Cloakroom" footnote hides. The padding, the plaques and the rank block lose a few pixels each, and the tally shrinks from 54px to 48px.

| Screen | Before (ANTE UP bottom) | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1280×720 | 848 (cut off, ledger 849–871 tall) | **617** (ledger 629 tall, ~90px to spare) |
| 1366×768 | 848 | **641** |
| 1920×1080 | 848 | 843 (full layout, unchanged) |
| 390×844 | ledger scrolls; ◈ AGAIN thumb on screen | unchanged: the ◈ AGAIN thumb sits at 744–822 |

Phones keep the long scrolling ledger on purpose: the gold ◈ AGAIN thumb coin is the phone's re-ante button and it's always on screen.
Screenshots: `shots/g1-before/` and `shots/g1-after/` (`ledger_<w>x<h>.jpg`). New check: `node probes/ledgerfit.js [label]`
dies after about 2 minutes, measures all four screens and exits 1 if ANTE UP (or the phone's AGAIN thumb) is off screen.

**Bonus fix: the boss bar came back over the ledger.** F1 hid `#bossBar` when the ledger opened, but the HUD tick kept running
after death. If the boss that killed you was still alive, the HUD put its purple bar straight back over "A RECORD IN BLOOD"
(visible in `shots/g1-before/ledger_1280x720.jpg`). The HUD now treats a dead player as "no boss" (`ui.js` boss-bar line).

### 2. The phone How to Play title stays on one line (F1 known issue 3)
At ≤600px wide the title drops to 21px with tighter letter-spacing and `nowrap`, so "♠ HOW TO PLAY ♠" is 239px wide on one line
at both 390 and 360px. The desktop title is unchanged (26px).

### 3. "THE HOUSE WAS BUILT BY" — left alone for the director's name, as asked.
