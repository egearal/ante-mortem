# H2 — FRESH EYES (new-player playtest + top friction fixes)

Branch `claude/h2-fresh-eyes`. `dist/` not committed. A3's first-night design (spawn ramp, bite ramp, 8-XP first
level, forgiven first RAGE, the three tips) is untouched. Veterans see no change in difficulty: no number moved.

## How I played it
`probes/h2play.js` (new) plays nights 1–3 on a **fresh save** the way a stranger meets them: menu → first night → first
slot → death → ledger → **THE MENU** button → next night. It:
- logs every banner as it is actually **shown** (not just requested), every coach tip and every feat/record toast, with game time;
- samples which overlays are on screen together (banner, coach tip, streak line, boss bar);
- screenshots the menu, 0:01 / 0:03 / 0:06 / 0:10 / 0:15 / 0:25 / 0:40 / 1:00, the first slot, the death and the ledger, every night.

Run it with `node probes/h2play.js <label> [--phone] [--nights N]`, which writes to `shots/h2/<label>[-phone]/` along with `log.txt`.
Baselines: `shots/h2/before/` (1280×720, 3 nights) and `shots/h2/before-phone/` (390×844, 2 nights). After-sets:
`shots/h2/after2/` (desktop) and `shots/h2/after-phone/`. Each fix also has a side-by-side pair `shots/h2/fixN_*_before|after.jpg`.

## What changed (and how it feels)
1. **The first minute speaks one language** (`ui.js`, H2 block at the end).
   - On a stranger's **first** night, the House holds its side-bets until the first slot's lesson has landed. That covers the gilt, secret, favor, bounty, dare and indulgence banners, the secret reveal card, and the feat toasts.
   - About 9s after the first slot closes (after "EVERY LEVEL PULLS THE SLOT"), or at 0:50 at the latest, it pays them out one by one, 1.3s apart.
   - Now the opening is just the move tip, "THE HOUSE OPENS GENTLY", FIRST BLOOD, then the reels. Around 0:27 the gilt starts raining in as a second wave of dopamine instead of noise on top of the lesson.
   - Nights 2–3 and veterans are unaffected.
2. **The first RAGE says SPACE** (`ascent.js`): "⚡ RAGE — PRESS SPACE TO CASH IT ⚡", once, on night one. Every later RAGE keeps the Ascent rule.
3. **Records only toast when there was a record to beat** (`fnStart` wrapper). Stats with no record before tonight are pre-marked as toasted.
4. **Clean felt behind the menu** (`core.js` frame + `ui.js` quitToMenu): no render in the menu state, and the canvas is cleared on THE MENU. This is also cheaper on phones.
5. **Plain chips for the first three nights** (`renderChips`):
   - The estate chip reads "5% OF THE HOUSE" (the weights move into its tooltip, and veterans still see them inline).
   - The duplicate RANK chip and BEST chip are hidden while `LT.runs<3`.
   - For everyone, chips now name their numbers: "INDEX 7%", "SECRETS 3/101", "BEST 5,967", "NEW WEAPON AT RANK 3: GALLOWS COIL" and "DAILY BLESSING +30 GILT".
6. **The Daily warns a stranger**: until the first night is played, the plaque shows "✦ NO GENTLE NIGHTS HERE — NEW? PLAY TONIGHT FIRST ✦" in soft red. It is still playable.
7. **First ledger**: no "#1 ALL-TIME" badges or stings on night one. Instead it says "✦ NIGHT ONE IS ON THE BOOKS — EVERY NUMBER HERE IS YOURS TO BEAT ✦". From night 2 the badges mean something and come back.
8. **Ledger fits 720p** (`styles.css`, H2 block): on wide-but-short screens (≥641px wide, ≤820px tall), the soul plaque and the rank/debt column sit side by side, and the portrait drops a size.
   - The whole ledger is now 671px tall, down from 855. ANTE UP, SHARE, BOOKS and MENU are all visible at 1280×720.
   - Phones and tall screens are unchanged.
9. **Day streak bug**: FIRST ANTE now keeps its own `LT.lastAnte` field. This is an additive field inside the existing save, and no save key changed.
10. Credit line now reads "the house was built by ege & claude ›" and opens the credits. Feat grammar is fixed ("Die once", "Play 1 run"), and the phone wave line no longer wraps.
