# Brief J0 — THE SMOOTH TABLE (frame rate)

Read CLAUDE.md and GAME_DESIGN.md first, then PLAN_FEED.md (the J0 section and the lane rules).

## Why
The director: "the fps is too low, 1 second in game is like 5 seconds real life". The analysis is done:
- The game is **fill-rate bound**. JS work is ~2ms per frame; audio, memory and hit-stop are ruled out.
- Baseline today (`node probes/fillarea.js dist/ante-mortem.html 300 150 high`, 1280×720, 300-enemy storm at 2:32):
  `screensPerFrame_drawImage 23.8 · screensPerFrame_fillRect 2.1 · bigDrawImagesPerFrame 20.9 · drawImagesPerFrame 663`.
  So about **26 full screens painted every frame**. At DPR 2 that is 4× the pixels.
- `frame()` caps `dt` at 0.05 (`src/core.js` ~3360). Any frame slower than 50ms runs the world in slow motion. That is the "1s = 5s" feeling.
- Two frame drivers: rAF and a 15ms `setInterval` (`core.js` ~3346-3355) with an 8ms dedup.

## Rules
- Branch `claude/j0-smooth-table` from the latest main. Touch only `src/core.js` render/loop/resize code, and `probes/`. Do not touch the XP gauge (`core.js` ~2030-2089, owned by J1) or any gameplay number.
- No visible change beyond sub-pixel softness in lights and sigil. Do not commit `dist/`.
- `npm run build`, `npm run probe -- --nights 2`, `node probes/daily-check.js` green, zero `_amErr`.
- Report in plain English in `notes/J0.md`: what it feels like, before/after numbers, what is left.

## The work, in order (measure after each step with `node probes/fillarea.js` on a fresh build)
1. **One frame driver.** rAF drives while visible. The `setInterval` only drives when `document.hidden` or when rAF has not fired for 100ms (embedded previews still run).
2. **Real time, not slow motion.** Raise the `dt` cap to 0.1 and sub-step `update` in slices of at most 0.05 (max 2 sub-steps) when a frame is long. Keep `update(dt*1.71*(slowT>0?0.35:1))` semantics exactly.
3. **One overlay bake.** `vignB.warm` (screen blend), `main` and `grd` (baked in `bakeVign`, ~1363-1406, drawn ~1979-2001) are static per (size, danger bucket, grade). Compose the static ones into a single canvas at bake time, draw once. `danger` and `heart` pulse, keep them separate. If the `screen` blend of `warm` cannot be pre-composed against an unknown floor, keep `warm` separate and merge `main`+`grd`.
4. **Sigil at half size.** `drawSigil` (~1295) draws four rotated ~1456px layers per frame, one with `'screen'`. Pre-compose static layers into one half-resolution canvas, upscale with smoothing; keep only the rotating part live. Also cull the sigil when it is off camera.
5. **Half-resolution light buffer.** Candle pools, sconce glows and the player halo (`lightSprite` draws) go into a VW/2×VH/2 offscreen canvas, composited once with `'lighter'`.
6. **A real low tier.** When `qLow` trips (`frameAvg>19`, ~3374): cap DPR at 1.5 first (not straight to 1), skip shafts and the sigil's live layer, halve light sprites. Thresholds unchanged.
7. Find any other full-screen pass you can merge or skip with `probes/fillarea.js` (it counts `bigDrawImagesPerFrame`).

## Gates
- **At least 40% fewer screens painted per frame** in the storm (drawImage + fillRect together, so ≤15.5 from ~26).
- **Game time keeps pace**: write `probes/pace.js`. On the virtual clock, step frames of 60ms and 100ms for 10 real seconds; `gt` must advance ≥95% of the expected game time (before: ~85% at 60ms, ~50% at 100ms).
- `npm run shots -- j0` next to `shots/` current sets; eyeball them (open the PNGs) and describe any difference in the report.
- `node probes/perf-ab.js dist/ante-mortem.html 2 300 high` before and after; put p50/p95 in the report.
