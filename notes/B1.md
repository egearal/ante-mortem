# B1 — DAILY DEBT

Branch `claude/b1-daily-debt`. No dist committed.

## What I built

### 1. One seeded night per UTC day
- **The seed** is the UTC date as `YYYYMMDD`. `DAILY DEBT #n` counts from **2026-11-05 = #1**, so the director's 9am game is #1. Today, 2026-11-04, is #0.
- **Random draws are seeded only in Daily Debt mode.** `core.js` has `DLY_RNG` and `rng(stream)`, plus the helpers `rrnd(stream,a,b)` and `rpick(stream,arr)`.
  - When `DLY_RNG` is null (every normal night), `rng()` just returns `Math.random()`, so normal mode behaves exactly as before.
  - On a daily night, each system draws from its own mulberry32 stream, seeded from `hash(day|stream)`. The streams are spawn, elite, slot, wheel, tarot, ascent, drop, loot, fate and combat.
  - Separate streams mean slot pull #3 is the same pull for every player, even if one player crit more often than another.
- **About 150 call sites were rerouted to `rng`**, tagged by stream:
  - spawns and waves, elites and bosses;
  - slot fate, reels and selector, dice and red-table curses;
  - wheel deck, ticker and tokens;
  - tarot draws and placement;
  - ascent crash points and prizes;
  - interactables, bounties, dares, blessings, scars and the egg;
  - loot scatter;
  - weapon crits and spread;
  - the death roll and its press increments.
- **Cosmetic randomness stays on `Math.random`:** particles, shake, sound pitch and menu dust.
- **Loot scatter is seeded** because coin and orb positions pull a real player (and the bot) around the floor. Without it, the runs drifted apart after about 30s.

### 2. The terms of the debt (`dlyTerms(day)`, its own seeded generator)
- **A name:** `THE <DEBT|MARKER|TAB|LIEN|…> OF THE <THIRD|THIRTEENTH|…> <WIDOW|CROUPIER|DROWNED SAILOR|…>`. That gives 1,600 combinations.
- **A fixed soul:** Vesper 3 times in 8, otherwise any other soul at 1 in 8 each. Locked souls are included, so players get a taste of what's sealed.
- **A fixed floor:** Pit 50%, Cellar 25%, Chapel 25%.
- **Modifiers:** every debt has one curse. Then there's a 55% chance of adding a boon and a 20% chance of adding a second curse.
- **The 14 modifiers all reuse existing mechanics:**
  - Curses: ☠ elites in pairs (NO MERCY), ❄ reels lean cold (new `P.fateMult`=0.5 in `dealFate`), ♞ a horde every wave (STAMPEDE), ♟ the pit runs heavy (CROWDED), ✕ the fountains run dry (DROUGHT), ♡ thin blood −25%, ⚡ the court runs fast (+10% via `_fmods.spd`), ⚖ the interest (+10% damage taken).
  - Boons: 🔥 hot reels (first 2 slots at red-table odds), ◈ a gilded floor (coins ×1.25), ☨ the cards come early (tarot ×2), ✦ a learned night (XP +15%), ♥ thick blood (+25), ⚜ loaded pockets (+60 coins).
- **Each modifier has** a glyph, a saturated colour in `GLYPH_COL['dly_<id>']`, and a description with real numbers, shown as a tooltip on the plaque and the ledger.
- **A fair field:** no rank perks, vault seals (`vlv`→0), vault contracts (`contractDone`→false), favors, run contracts, indulgences or THE EQUAL.
  - The in-run vault door never opens.
  - The A3 first-night easing is off.
  - The whole armory is open to everyone, so slot pools are the same regardless of rank.
  - Indulgences the player has bought are **not consumed**; they wait for their next normal night.

### 3. The menu plaque
- **Where:** on desktop (≥900×600, A1's two-column menu) it closes the right-hand parlor column under OPTIONS. On phones and short screens it sits under PLAY TONIGHT and the loadout line. `dlyPlace()` re-homes it on resize.
- **What it shows:**
  - "☀ DAILY DEBT #n", with a live countdown to the next debt (UTC midnight);
  - the debt's name;
  - soul · floor · modifiers, each in its colour;
  - TODAY (UNPAID / time + track / FORFEIT), BEST, and 🔥 STREAK.
- **The button** reads ☀ PAY TODAY'S DEBT, then ☀ PRACTICE THE DEBT once today is played. The whole plaque is clickable and breathes a gold glow until today is paid.
- **At UTC midnight** the plaque re-posts itself with a bell toll.
- **Which attempt is scored:** the first attempt of the day is SCORED, and it is spent the moment the bell rings, so reloading doesn't reroll it. Quitting mid-run records the time as it stood. Later attempts are PRACTICE.
- **ANTE UP — AGAIN after a daily** replays the same debt as practice. PLAY TONIGHT is always a normal night.
- **Persistence** uses a new key, **`am_daily_v1`**: `{v:1, days:{YYYYMMDD:{n,s,t,lvl,kills,track,done,pt,pl,fled}}, bestT, bestDay, streak, lastDay}`. It keeps about 90 days and goes through the same `store` as everything else. No existing key is touched.

### 4. Ledger and share
- **The DAILY DEBT band:**
  - On screens ≥1100px it's pinned top-left beside the ledger, because the panel is already full.
  - On narrower screens it goes at the top of the panel. The panel gets `overflow-y:auto` only on daily deaths.
  - It shows #n with a SCORED or PRACTICE tag, the name and terms, and the **minute-by-minute track**: ♠ survived · ♦ a pair · ★ a jackpot · ⚔ a boss fell · ☠ the end · ♛ the full night.
  - It also shows today's time, best ever, streak, and the payout breakdown.
- **The share line** (A3's `a3Line`, `a3Night` and `a3Card` are wrapped, not edited):
  ```
  ♠ ANTE MORTEM · DAILY DEBT #12 · survived 9:41 · lvl 16
  ♠♠♦♠★♠⚔♠♠☠
  https://egearal.github.io/ante-mortem/
  ```
- **The card** gets a gilt ribbon "☀ DAILY DEBT #n · <name> ☀" over the night line, and the coloured track glyphs at the bottom. See `shots/b1/b1_share_card.png`.

### 5. Rewards (fake gilt only)
- **On a scored daily**, on top of the normal score/40:
  - debt 20,
  - plus 4 per full minute survived (max 60),
  - plus streak 5 × (streak−1) (max 50),
  - plus 25 for a new personal best.
  - That's 20 at the bare minimum and 155 at the most.
- **Practice pays nothing extra.**
- **A new daily best** gets the full show: bell plus an ascending peal (`dlyFanfare`), bigWin "NEW DAILY BEST", confetti in the ledger, a gold flash, an announce, and the band's ✦ A NEW DAILY BEST ✦ slamming in.
- **Sounds** are synthesized: `dlyBell` is a low toll with a gold shimmer, played on start, on the ledger and at the midnight re-post. There's also a soft tick for each minute mark.

## How it feels
- **At the menu,** there's a glowing plaque under the big gold button: *THE MARKER OF THE THIRD CROUPIER — ☾ The Widow · ♣ The Cellar · ☠ elites in pairs*, with a clock ticking down to tomorrow's debt.
- **Paying it:** you click, a funeral bell tolls in the counting house, and the House announces the debt, its terms, and then "THIS ONE IS SCORED — ONE ATTEMPT". Every minute you live, a soft chime writes a mark.
- **At death,** a plaque pinned beside the ledger shows your night as a row of suits ending in a red skull. Gilt pours in, and if you beat your best, the jackpot sirens go.
- **Sharing:** the button gives you a Wordle line your friends can compare against, because they played the exact same night.

## Verified
- **`npm run build`:** clean. The syntax gate now counts 14 scripts.
- **`node probes/daily-check.js`** (new, exit 0), on the built file. It runs five fresh browsers:
  - **two daily runs of the same seed with the same scripted bot inputs** give an **identical first 150 game-seconds**: 176/176 spawns (time, type, x, y), both slot results (kind, id, match count), and the slot fates;
  - the next day's seed diverges;
  - two normal nights diverge (they still use `Math.random`);
  - daily armed / normal not.
  - **I used 150s rather than 60s** because a no-ease night pulls its first slot at about 1:20, so 60s would have included zero slot results. Spawns match over 60s too, a subset of the check.

- **`--nights 5`:** exit 0, zero errors, deaths 4:22 / 5:43 / 3:57 / 5:38 / 13:07 (median 5:38). Both runs are within the v4.0 baseline band. Normal mode still draws from `Math.random` through `rng()`.

## Known issues / notes
- **Spawn positions depend on the viewport** (`Math.max(VW,VH)`, as before), so players with different screen sizes get the same types, counts and timing at slightly different distances. Bit-identical nights also need the same frame timing. Those are the limits of a dt-based sim. Slot pull #k, wheel spin #k, tarot draw #k and so on are identical for everyone regardless.
- **The ☀ glyph renders as a sun-like asterisk** in the Cinzel fallback on desktop. It's safe, but looks less like a sun than the emoji would.
- **Achievements, secrets, the Index and rank marks still accrue on dailies.** They are progress, not power, and favors don't apply in the night.
- **The normal-mode spawn curve is untouched.** Medians move between runs because of RNG noise; A3 saw 3:14–5:25 for the same build.
