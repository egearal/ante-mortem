# J1 — THE FEED (replaces RAGE) + weapons sooner

Branch `claude/v2-improve` (built in one session with J2; J3 merged after).

## How it feels
- When the XP bar fills it doesn't punish you any more, it gets **hungry**.
  - The gauge swells (20px tall, wider), and you hit +50% harder and faster.
  - The bar drains faster the longer it lives. Every soul and coin you grab feeds it.
- Four jewels sit on the gauge's top edge and light one after another:
  - green while it eats;
  - **blue at 3s — WARM ◆**;
  - **violet at 7s — HOT ◆◆**;
  - **gold ★ at 12s — THE FEAST**, where the reels can only land epic or legendary.
- Each jewel lands with a rarity sting, a colored flash, a ring and a banner ("◆◆ THE REELS RUN HOT ◆◆").
- **SPACE cashes it any time**, with no penalty. The next slot opens dressed in the tier's color: a blue glow, a violet glow, or a breathing gold machine with a choir for the feast. THE ODDS plate retitles itself and strikes out the rarities the feast forbids.
- Get greedy and let it starve, and a quarter of your XP is gone (the bar reads 75%). You keep playing: no coin sweep, no damage-taken penalty.
- **Weapons come sooner:** the 2nd, 5th, 8th … slot is the Arsenal (was every 4th). Your second level-up is a new weapon.

## What changed
- `ascent.js`: `startOvercharge` / `feedTick` / `feedTierUp` / `cashRage` (+ `cashFeed`) / `feedStarve`, plus `FEED_T` / `FEED_COL`.
  - Gone: `rageAscend`, `rageDrop`, `ascTime`, `ascPend`, the gate-by-rage, the ×1.5 damage taken, the coin-loss cash and the bonus-level cash.
  - Token pity window is now 45-65s.
- `slot.js`: `FEED_W`, `slotFeedTier` → `slotTierNow` (also `pending` entries carry `{ft}`) and `slotWeights()` at every reel draw.
  - `weightedByRarity` now honours zero weights. If a pool has nothing allowed, it falls back to its richest bucket.
  - A FEAST on a weapon turn with no epic/legendary weapon unlocked becomes a skill pull, and the weapon waits one slot. A fresh save has only common-rare weapons, so otherwise the feast would have handed out a rare.
  - Paytable and machine are tinted by tier.
- `core.js`: the gauge (jewels, tier gradients, the label fitted to narrow screens and centered over the gauge so it never runs under the BET coin). XP feeds ×0.8. Streak hint fixed to /4 (it said /3 but paid /4).
- Text sweep: HOW TO PLAY, first-night coach + forgiveness (the House cashes the first feed once if it drops under 30), Bar Stoker, rank 80, the Banker's Ghost (seat counter now counts HOT-or-richer cashes; key `rage` unchanged).
- Probe bot holds the feed to WARM (or `--feed-tier N`) before cashing.
