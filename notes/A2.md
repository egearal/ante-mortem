# A2 — GILT NOIR: THE MACHINE AND THE HALL

Branch `claude/a2-gilt-noir-machine`. Presentation only: no odds, fate, economy, save-key or gameplay
numbers were changed. `dist/` is not committed.

## What it feels like now

**The slot is a cast-brass machine.** A gradient brass bezel with an engraved inner lip, four
screw-head bolts and a ♠ seal medallion on the crown (☠ at the red table, ⚔ for the arsenal). The
marquee is backlit oxblood glass in a brass frame. The reels sit in a recess cut into the metal,
lacquered drums that darken toward the top and bottom like a cylinder turning away from you, behind
a curved glass pane with a resting reflection.

- **The lever pulls.** Every spin, the chrome arm swings down past its brass hub (the ball drops
  about 150px and swells toward you), springs back, and a light sweep runs across the glass. It has
  its own sound: a spring stretch, a ratchet rattle and the hub knocking.
- **Reels clunk.** Each stop overshoots a hair, the latch catches and it settles with a tiny bounce.
  The drum jolts down 4px and flashes. New sound: a dull iron thud with a brass click on top, layered
  under the existing F-A-C chord.
- **The near-miss tease.** When reels one and two match, the last reel no longer just stops. It
  creeps the final row with a ratchet that ticks slower each notch. If a matching symbol is within
  two rows, the reel drags it onto the payline, hangs there (the rails flare, the arrows jab, the
  room gasps), then wobbles one row past. A reel dealt a triple crawls home instead and the latch
  drops on it. All of this happens after the result is already decided; it only times the reveal.
- **The payline pays.** The rails strobe in the landed rarity's color with a rising brass
  glissando (more steps per tier). A PAIR presses a "◆ PAIR ◆" stamp onto the glass, strobes the
  marquee and throws confetti. A TRIPLE stamps "♛ TRIPLE ♛", runs the jackpot glow, makes the crown
  medallion throb, then follows up with sparks, a flash, bells, a second confetti burst and coin
  rain. All of that sits on top of the existing bigWin/rarityLandFX.
- **The red table** recasts the brass as blood-copper, with a ☠ seal. Its drums now fill the wider
  window instead of floating in empty felt.
- **Bug fixed along the way (an old one):** when the fate nudge pushed a stop above row 0 of the
  strip, the payline showed the empty space above the strip, so the reel looked blank. It now rides
  the strip's second copy. Same symbol, visual fix only.

**Notices never overlap (THE LANES).** The announce banner, the secret card, the House's tips
(coach marks) and feat toasts now share one stacked column (`#a2Notes`). Items in a column can't
overlap each other. The column is then parked wherever the open table isn't:
- In play: top-center, as before.
- Slot, wheel or ascent open: above the table if there's room, otherwise beside it (left of the slot,
  since the odds card is on the right), otherwise below it, and at the very top only as a last resort.
- Feat toasts keep their usual bottom-left corner unless it would land on the open table; then
  they join the column too.
- Death ledger: in the right margin on wide screens. On narrow screens the column sits at the top and
  the ledger slides down to make room (the ledger layer now scrolls).
- Banners scale their font to fit the lane (14–30px) and wrap with balanced lines when even 14px
  can't hold them. The phone clipping in `shots/before/m2_play.jpg` is gone.
- A found secret used to show both a banner and its own flip card with the same name. The duplicate
  banner is dropped when the card is showing that secret.

**HUD as engraved plaques.** The coin, kill and timer readouts are dark plaques with brass rims and
tabular numerals. HP is blood in a brass vial with a ♥ medallion cap. The level plate and relic
sockets have brass rings and glass catch-lights, and rarity glows are kept. The canvas LEVEL CHARGE
gauge sits in a cast-brass frame with rivets and a catch-light (gradients are cached, so it costs
nothing per frame). Nothing grew over the play area.

**The death ledger** is a bronze-bound page that fits 1280×720 (it was 936px tall and spilled off
both edges). The hierarchy reads top to bottom:
1. The verdict, then an engraved epitaph strip: "☠ TAKEN BY SWARM · SURVIVED 3:06 · LEVEL 3".
2. THE TALLY: the score in struck-gold numerals, the gilt earned and the luck grade.
3. The night as six engraved plaques (with the #1 ALL-TIME badges) and the run ledger.
4. What's next: the rank bar and the next unlock, a big struck-gold ANTE UP plaque, and BOOKS and
   MENU side by side.

On phones it collapses to one column and scrolls.

**The hall (menu)** is no longer clipped at 1280×720. The title used to sit half off the top and the
doors ran off the bottom. On screens 900px and wider it's now a two-column composition: title, chips,
rank and PLAY TONIGHT on the left; the six doors stand in a column on the right as bronze-rimmed
velvet plaques. PLAY TONIGHT is struck gold with a dark keyline. Below 900px the menu keeps its
single-column flow and now scrolls instead of clipping. The existing juice spec (hover lift, sheen,
gold edge bar) is kept and extended.

Also: the Wheel of Souls ticker window has a brass rim; the Feats/Index/Ledger/Vault/Books/Options
panels and the pause card have a bronze binding.
