# A3 — THE FIRST NIGHT + THE SHARE CARD

Branch `claude/a3-first-night`. No dist committed.

## What I built

### 1. The first three nights
Who gets it: a player whose lifetime `LT.runs` is 3 or fewer (I only read it, never write it) **and** whose `RANK.rank` is below 8. Anyone with rank 8 or higher is treated as a regular from their first night, and so is `--rank 20` in the probe. `window._fnOff=true` turns it off for testing. There are **no new save keys**: the lessons are stored as `LT.coach` flags, the same way every other coach mark is.

- **Spawn ramp:** wave size and horde size are multiplied by `fnEase()`. It starts at 0.55 at the opening bell and smoothsteps up to 1.0 by 1:45 (`FN.ramp=105`). The wait between waves is stretched by 1/ease², so it starts about 3.3× longer and goes back to normal.
- **Bite ramp:** enemies that spawn during the ramp hit for 0.6× to 1.0× damage, rising with the spawn ramp. After 1:45 everything is back on the true curve.
- **The first slot comes fast:** the first level needs 8 XP instead of 28. Until that level, souls count 1.5× in both places the game applies `xpMult`, so each kill is worth 2 XP. The multiplier is reverted exactly when the first level lands. From level 2 the curve is unchanged.
- **The first RAGE is forgiven:** if the player doesn't press SPACE before the bar falls under 90, the House cashes it for them once per night: "◈ THE HOUSE CASHED FOR YOU — THIS ONCE · NEXT TIME, SPACE ◈". Without this, a stranger who never pressed SPACE lost half their XP to the first RAGE and never saw a slot.
- **A night-opening line:** "THE HOUSE OPENS GENTLY — FOR NOW" on night 1, "NIGHT 2 OF 3 — THE HOUSE STILL GOES EASY" on night 2, and on night 3 a red "YOUR LAST GENTLE NIGHT — TOMORROW THE HOUSE PLAYS FOR KEEPS". New players know the gentleness won't last.
- **The Death Roll is unchanged:** the first death still triggers PRESS FOR YOUR LIFE as usual.

### 2. Three tips (night 1 only)
Each tip shows once, in the existing `coachMark` ('THE HOUSE EXPLAINS') toast. None of them repeats what an existing coach mark already says. They never block play, and any key dismisses them.
- `fn_move` (0.9s): "WASD or ARROWS to walk — your bolt aims itself at the nearest dead · never stand still". On touch devices it says "DRAG to walk".
- `fn_space` (the moment the first RAGE starts): "SPACE IS EVERY BET — press it NOW to cash the RAGE · reels, dice, wheel: all SPACE".
- `fn_slot` (about 5s after the first slot closes): "EVERY LEVEL PULLS THE SLOT — what lands is yours for the night: weapons, skills, sometimes a curse". The existing `slot` mark explains how to stop the reels; this one explains what the slot is for.

### 3. The share card
- A **♠ SHARE YOUR NIGHT ♠** plaque is pinned to the top-right of the death layer and glows gently.
- I put it on `#gameover` itself, not inside the ledger panel. On a 1280×720 screen the existing ledger panel is about 1020px tall with no scroll, so a button inside it would be off-screen. Pinning it outside the panel also means A2's ledger restyle can't move or break it.
- Clicking it does three things:
  - copies `♠ ANTE MORTEM — survived 7:56 · level 14 · 2 jackpots · taken by HUSK · https://egearal.github.io/ante-mortem/` to the clipboard (falling back to `execCommand`);
  - on a touch device with `navigator.share`, opens the share sheet with the PNG attached when `canShare` allows files;
  - shows a modal with the card, the copied line, **⬇ SAVE THE CARD** (a PNG download) and **✕ CLOSE**.
- The card is a 1200×630 canvas in the GILT NOIR look:
  - velvet radial background, a faint damask lattice, two additive candle pools with candles, and a vignette;
  - a double gilt frame with ♠♥♦♣ corners;
  - a gold ANTE MORTEM title, then "night № · soul · rank title";
  - the survival time in a huge glowing gold numeral;
  - four plaques: LEVEL / SLAIN / JACKPOTS (triples, `RUN.pulls._tr3`) / BEST STREAK;
  - "TAKEN BY <killer>" in blood red, the best pull's rarity in its own color, and the URL with "no real money — only your soul".
- **The card never shows coins, gilt or score.**
- Sounds: chip stack plus casino ding on click; coin pling, tingle and sparks when the card is ready; vault-buy chime on save.

### 4. Extra celebration in the first minute (first nights)
- **FIRST BLOOD:** gold float text, double gilt ring, gold burst, a small gold flash and shake, casino ding plus two coin plings.
- **FIRST LEVEL:** "♛ FIRST LEVEL — THE REELS ARE YOURS ♛", seraph chord, coin rain, gold flash and rings, and confetti in the slot as it opens.

## How it feels
In the first night the floor fills slowly. The first corpse goes off like a small jackpot. Four or five kills later the RAGE bar flares, the House tells you SPACE is every bet, and if you fumble it the House cashes for you, once. Then the reels drop in with confetti. All of that happens in about the first 20 seconds of game clock. By 1:45 the House is playing at full strength, and from night 4 (or rank 8) it's the old brutal game.

## Numbers (headless, virtual clock, game clock)
**Head-to-head, 10 fresh-save nights each, same harness:** the probe bot was run on the baseline v4.0 build and on the A3 build. In the probe every night is a first night.

| | Baseline v4.0 | A3 |
|---|---|---|
| First slot (median) | **1:10** (67–75s) | **0:19.5** (10–25s) |
| Median survival | 4:21 | **5:04** |
| Survival range | 2:49–7:34 | 2:52–13:08 (2 nights reached THE HOUSE at 13:05) |
| HP at 2:00 | 33–130 | 63–88 |
| Level at 3:00 | 3 | 4 |

**Official `npm run probe -- --nights 3`** on the built file: exit 0, zero errors, deaths at 2:54, 3:14 and 3:56 (median 3:14, above the ≥2:30 target). Earlier 3-night runs on the same build gave medians of 5:19 and about 5:25. Nights vary a lot, and n=3 can't settle the comparison; the 10-vs-10 table above is the fair one.

**Veteran, `npm run probe -- --nights 5 --rank 20`:** exit 0, zero errors, deaths at 3:36, 3:52, 5:15, 5:35 and 6:14 (median 5:15). The baseline median was 5:36. The first slot came at about 1:10, unchanged from baseline. The veteran code path is the same as before: `fnEase()` and `fnBite()` return 1, the first level needs 28 XP, and there are no tips.

**No-input "player"** (never moves and never presses SPACE outside the gambles): baseline dies at about 0:25 and never sees a slot. A3 dies at about 0:28 and **sees the first slot at about 0:19**. A stationary player still loses, and the move tip is aimed at exactly them. But they now see the hook before they go. The brief's "~8s no-input death" figure is from an older batch; on v4.0 I measured about 25s.

**Lesson timeline from a live first night:**
- 0:01.5: move tip
- 0:02.4: "THE HOUSE OPENS GENTLY"
- 0:05: FIRST BLOOD
- 0:12.7: RAGE, then the slot opens with FIRST LEVEL confetti
- 0:21: "EVERY LEVEL PULLS THE SLOT" tip

The SPACE tip didn't show in this run only because the bot cashes RAGE on the same frame it starts; a human sees it. `xpMult` was back at exactly 1 after the first level.

**Share card:** tested headless at 1280×720 and at 390×844. The line was copied to the clipboard (read back on the 390 run: exact text), the card PNG is 1200×630, and there were zero console errors. `shots/a3/` is the after-set; compare `d5_death.jpg` with `shots/before/`.

## Files touched (merge notes)
- `src/ranks.js`: new block appended at the end (`/* ==== A3 — THE FIRST NIGHT ==== */`) with FN, fnOn, fnEase, fnBite, fnStart, fnTick and fnFirstLevel.
- `src/marker.js`: new block appended at the end (`/* ==== A3 — THE SHARE CARD ==== */`) with a3Night, a3Line, a3Copy, a3Card and a3ShareInit, which runs at load.
- `src/core.js`: six one-line hooks, each tagged `A3` or calling fn*:
  - in `spawnEnemy` the dmgS line gets `*fnBite()`;
  - in `startWave` the size and horde lines get `*fnEase()`;
  - the wave-gap `else` in update gets `/Math.pow(fnEase(),2)`;
  - `fnTick(dt)` sits just before the RAGE decay block;
  - `fnFirstLevel()` sits just before `openNextSlot()` at the end of gainXP;
  - `fnStart()` sits just before `state='playing'` in startGame.
- `styles.css`: one block at the end headed `/* ==== A3 ==== */`, with every rule scoped to `#a3Share`.
- No changes to index.html or to any save key.

Risky spots:
- `fnTick` must stay **before** the `if(P.ovT>0){ P.ovAge+=dt;` block. If it moves after it, the forgiveness never fires.
- If A4 adds a pause/codex button near the top-right on the death screen, `#a3Share` sits at top 14px / right 54px (40px on phones, to clear the ♪ icon).

## Known issues
- The existing ledger panel overflows 720px-tall screens: the score is cut at the top and THE MENU button is unreachable, with no scroll. It's not in my lane; it's A2's ledger. A `max-height:100vh; overflow-y:auto` on `#gameover .panel` would fix it.
- Clipboard writes in a non-secure context fall back to `execCommand`. If both fail, the modal says "COPY THE LINE BELOW" and shows the line as selectable text.
- SPACE on the death screen still means skip / ANTE UP while the share modal is open. That's intended: the skip law wins.
