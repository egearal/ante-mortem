# C1 — THE PAIRINGS (weapon evolutions)

Branch `claude/c1-pairings`. `dist/` not committed.

## What it feels like now

Max out a weapon while you own its one true skill, at any level, and the next time the bar fills
the slot doesn't deal you a normal pull. The machine goes gilt and the marquee reads
**♛ THE PAIRING ♛**. A cathedral bell tolls under the felt and the lever drops by itself. The reels
roll down: your weapon latches on the left, your skill on the right, and the middle reel creeps
in with a ratchet onto a pulsing gold "?". The two parents tear loose from their reels, fly into
the middle one, and the evolved glyph is born there. The name slams across the screen (bigWin
tier 4), A2's stamp presses "♛ PAIRING ♛" onto the glass, and the rails strobe gold. On top of
that comes the new fanfare: D minor walking up to a D major it has no right to, with a seraph
choir, confetti, coin rain, sparks and aftershocks. The odds card turns into the recipe card
(weapon MAX + skill = ? → the evolved name). Then the card states exactly what you're getting,
and you press **♛ CLAIM THE PAIRING**. SPACE skips the ceremony to the birth; a second SPACE goes
straight to the card. The pairing is a **bonus**: your normal pull follows right after it.

The evolved relic sits in your weapon slot with a gold ♛ pip and a pulsing gilt ring.

**Discovery loop.**
- Index: a new **♛ THE PAIRINGS** section right under the Arsenal. Unknown recipes show "??? + ???".
  Once you've ever maxed the parent weapon, the tile turns dashed and whispers a vague hint (e.g.
  "The knives ask for quicker hands…"). Discovered recipes show the full recipe and a lifetime count.
- Slot cards: a weapon about to hit max says **"♛ PAIRS WITH SOMETHING…"**. It names the partner
  once you know the recipe, and says "PAIRING READY — NEXT PULL" if you already own the partner.
  A skill that completes a known recipe says "♛ COMPLETES …".
- Maxing a pairing weapon announces it once per run.
- Sealing a new recipe announces "📖 A NEW RECIPE IS INSCRIBED IN THE INDEX".

## Verified
- `npm run build` clean (13-script gate).
- `npm run probe -- --nights 3`, final build: exit 0, zero errors, deaths 3:40 / 5:44 / 5:03,
  median **5:02**, levels 4–6, inside the 4:50–6:40 baseline. The bot never maxes a weapon, so the
  normal curve is untouched by design.
- **New probe `node probes/pairing.js [--only id,id] [--shots label] [--night]`.** On the built
  file, for each pairing:
  1. The bot plays (god mode) to 2:00.
  2. The probe maxes the weapon, grants the skill and measures 15s of base DPS.
  3. It queues a level-up and checks that the slot opens in stage `pairing`, then hands off the bot
     and checks the ceremony reaches `pairoffer` on its own clock.
  4. It claims, then checks that `P.evo`, `IDX.pr` and `LT.pairings` are set.
  5. It measures 15s of evolved DPS.
  6. It packs 300 immortal dummies around the player and measures real ms per frame (update +
     render every frame, q=high) and controlled DPS, before and after the evolution.

  Result: **all 10 OK, 0 errors**. Every evolution's frame cost is within run-to-run noise of its
  base weapon. Headless software rendering sits at ~50–75ms avg at 300 enemies with or without
  evolutions, which is environment cost, not this feature. Arena DPS multipliers over the base
  weapon after the balance pass:

  | Pairing | Arena DPS vs base weapon |
  |---|---|
  | Ossuary (on top of a strong bolt) | ×2 |
  | Thousand Cuts | ×4 |
  | Bloody Hand | ×7–8 |
  | Stacked Deck | ×7–12 |
  | Iron Maiden | ×6–9 |
  | Eye | ×23–40 |
  | Garden | ×33–47 |
  | Rat King | ×48–58 |
  | Crematorium | ×60–65 |
  | Russian Roulette | ×75–150 |

  Live-fight ratios are ×1–×20 (noisy: they depend on how many foes are around). Every number
  scales only with P.damage and crit, never with enemy HP. Enemy HP grows +66% per minute (×2.3 at
  2:00, ×5 at 6:00), so an evolution claimed around 2:00 flattens the floor for a couple of minutes
  and is then reeled back in.
- `--night` (evolution claimed at 2:00, god mode then turned off): the bot died between 2:23 and
  13:05, median about 5:00. It's strong, but it doesn't trivialize the night.
- Index persistence: `pr`/`pm` survive save → reload (checked against `localStorage`
  `am_index_v1`). No existing key or field changed.
- Shots in `shots/c1-after/`: `<id>_cer1100` (spin), `_cer2900` (parents fusing), `_cer3700`
  (birth), `_offer` (claim card), `_play` (the evolution in the field) for ossuary, cuts, hand,
  stacked, russian and eye, plus `index_pairings.jpg`.

## Known issues
- **Ascent hang (not reproduced, probably pre-existing).** One early `npm run probe` night on this
  branch sat in `state==='crash'` (the Ascent) for 26k frames ("alive at 2:32").
  - It did not reproduce in 8 further nights on this branch or in 6 nights on origin/main.
  - C1 code never runs in the crash state.
  - Flagging it for B4 / the orchestrator as a possible bot-vs-Ascent stall.
- **Probe numbers are noisy.** Live DPS varies with how many foes are near. Arena DPS uses
  immortal dummies, so kill-triggered effects (rat breeding, executions) are under-counted and AoE
  is flattered.
- **Ossuary is the hardest recipe.** Extra Ante is legendary-rarity.
- **Russian Roulette is rank 27+**, because the Cylinder unlocks at rank 27. The other nine use
  night-one weapons.
