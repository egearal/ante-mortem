# J3 — THE LESSON

## What it feels like
A stranger opens the game and, under PLAY TONIGHT, a gold pill breathes: **✦ NEW HERE? TAKE THE LESSON · 2 MINUTES ✦**. One tap (or **T**, or the new **✒ THE LESSON** door in the parlor column) and the House opens a private table: the floor is quiet, a gilt plaque hangs under the HUD — *LESSON 1 / 6 · ♞ WALK* — six little diamond pips in the colors of what's coming, and a gold ring glows a few steps away with an arrow pointing at it. Walk in: chime, rings, *✦ WALK — LEARNED ✦*, and the next plaque drops in.

Then the crawlers come (only the slow ones). Reap eight. The bar fills, and the plaque talks you through THE FEED live — *FEEDING ◆◇◇ · WARM ◆ · 2.1s TO HOT ◆◆* — until you cash it with SPACE and the slot slams open with the reels running warm. Spin, take it. A purple case lands beside you with 20 coins in your pocket: snap, spin, brake — it pays 90 coins. Then the green gate: ride it and cash (or crash and taste a curse — either way you learned). The House clears the floor in a ring of gold, **LESSON LEARNED** in huge gilt letters, confetti, coin rain, the seraph chord, *♠ THE HOUSE IS OPEN — PLAY TONIGHT ♠*, and you're back at the menu with a ✓ on the door.

You cannot die (blood never drops under 40%, hits still flash and sting), nothing counts against you, and the lesson does not spend one of your three gentle first nights.

## What changed
- New `src/lesson.js` (loaded last). The six steps, the `#lessonBar` banner with smart placement, the menu door/chip/hotkey, the persistence (`am_lesson_v1 = {done, n}`), and every hook as a wrapper inside lesson.js.
- One line each where the brief allowed it: `startGame` → `lessonBegin()` (core.js), `spawnBoss` guard (core.js), guards at the top of `bump`, `awardMarks`, `recCheck`, `secret` (ranks.js), the pause quit pill reads **LEAVE THE LESSON** (ui.js). All guards read `window.LESSON`: the build concatenates every file into ONE script, so a bare `LESSON` from an earlier file would hit the temporal dead zone.
- `index.html` script tag, `styles.css` (#lessonBar, chip, door), CLAUDE.md rule 4 (15 scripts, lesson.js last + the TDZ warning), GAME_DESIGN.md (✒ THE LESSON section + key), README.
- `scripts-check.js` counts files dynamically, so it needed no change (it now reports 15).
- New `probes/lesson.js` + `shots/lesson/` (22 shots: every step, the pause, the finish, the menu before/after, desk and phone).

Choices I made:
- The brief said refresh `P.inv` every frame; that would stop hits from flashing at all, so instead blood is floored at 40% and `die()` is refused while the lesson runs.
- The lesson always seats Vesper in THE PIT with no contracts, indulgences or stake. The Banker soul deletes wheel cases and the Ballroom drops chandeliers, which would break the lesson. The player's picks are put back the moment the night starts, and indulgences are kept for a real night.
- `LT` is snapshotted before the lesson and restored after it, so kills and coins never reach the lifetime ledger and real coach tips still fire on night one. The only trace left is the day's +15 "first ante" gilt.
- XP can't fill the bar except during step 3, so slots never interrupt the other lessons. The wheel is rigged to land 90 COINS, so no Ascent jump interrupts the wheel lesson. The case and gate land 190px away and never expire.
- The banner on the floor sits under the HUD and pushes the notice column (#a2Notes) down under itself. With a table open it finds a free margin: beside the table on desktop, under it on a phone (left of BET/CASH). It drops to a compact plate, then to a single engraved line if space is tight, and folds away if nothing fits or the table is still growing in.
