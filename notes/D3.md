# D3 — THE BALLROOM (a fourth floor)

Branch `claude/d3-fourth-floor`. New floor entry + its own events, art and sound. No souls/bosses/evolutions touched.

## What it feels like now
You step onto smoked mirror glass. The floor is violet-black, cut into panes by faint silver-gilt inlay, and where
four panes meet, a wine-red diamond. Thin reflection streaks run across the glass like the room's light catching it.
In the middle of the hall a great rosette is set into the floor — a 16-point compass star in silver on a wine disc —
lit from above by a cool pool of chandelier light. The pit's red cracks and gargoyles are gone; this room is polished.
Under it all a music box plays a waltz in A minor, very far away, with the odd chime of crystal overhead.

**♫ THE WALTZ** — every 90 seconds the music box swells into a full waltz (oom-pah-pah, a melody line, a rising
arpeggio), the announcer calls `♫ THE FLOOR WALTZES — TURNING SUNWISE ♫` and the floor *turns*. For 16 seconds
everything — the court, the coins, the soul shards, and you — drifts in a slow circle around the spot where the music
found you. Dashed silver rings and wine chevrons spin on the floor to show the direction. Ride the current and you
outrun the horde; walk against it and you slow to a crawl while they're carried into your guns. The direction
alternates each waltz (sunwise / widdershins).

**✦ CHANDELIER CRASH** — every ~30s a chain creaks, `✦ THE CHANDELIER SWAYS — STAND CLEAR ✦`, and a silver-and-crystal
chandelier hangs swaying, translucent, over a circle on the floor: a shadow that darkens, a white ring, and a red dashed
ring tightening onto it. Crystal tinkles at 1.1s, a chain *snaps* at 2.15s, and at 2.6s it falls — the sprite drops
and slams. Everything under it dies (bosses take 12% instead; THE HOUSE is merely STAGGERED for 2.6s), white and silver
glass sprays everywhere, the screen shakes, and a pile of gold coins lies in the wreck (the bent rings and glass stay on
the floor for 20s). Crush 7+ and it's a jackpot banner — `CHANDELIER ×9`, 14+ `CHANDELIER ×n` tier 2, 25+ `CRYSTAL MASSACRE`.
The drop leads the target: it lands where a cluster of the court *will* be as they walk toward you, and never on top
of you (always ≥ R+30 away) — you only die under it if you walk in. **During THE HOUSE (13:00+) the drops come every
11–15s and hunt *you*** — and the House itself can be lured under one for a 2.6s stagger. Stand inside when it lands
and you're dead (Death Roll / Phoenix / second chance still apply).

**◇ MIRROR TWINS** — every ~30s one edge of the screen silvers over (a bright line and a pale band),
`◇ THE MIRRORS STIR ◇`, a rising glass glissando, and a second later the court's reflections step out of that edge:
the on-screen enemies are mirrored across the edge line, same types, silver-bodied, half the blood, a silver ghost-glow
underneath. They shatter into silver shards with a glass *tink* when they die. Off during THE HOUSE.

Unlock: **SURVIVE 10:00** (record minutes ≥ 10) or MEET THE HOUSE once, or (when B2 lands) clear stake 1.
Card glyph ♕, color #d9a3c0. No flat stat modifiers — the floor's economy is the chandelier.

## Verified
- `npm run build` clean.
- `npm run probe -- --nights 3` (default floor): 13:05 / 4:14 / 8:50, median 8:49, **0 errors**.
- `npm run probe -- --nights 3 --floor ballroom`: 0 errors (several runs).
- Death-curve A/B on the bot (virtual clock, same harness): **Pit 8 nights median 5:23 · Ballroom 16 nights median 5:13**
  (range 2:51–12:07), 0 errors, **0 player chandelier deaths** across ~70 ballroom nights.
  How I got there: v1 ran ~4:20 median. Isolation batches (mirrors off / waltz off / chandeliers off) showed the
  chandeliers were the cost — not by crushing the bot but by the bot panic-dodging into the court. Fixes: night drops
  land beside you (≥R+30), bot dodge softened. Coins were 2–3× the Pit's; trimmed (no coin modifier, wreck pile
  3 + 1 per two bodies, cap 20). Ballroom nights still earn more coins than the Pit (more kills) — intended as the
  reward of a late-unlock floor; balance pass may trim.
- God-mode night to 13:06 on the Ballroom: THE HOUSE spawns, drops keep coming, no errors (`f8`).
- `npm run phone`: green.
- **Perf**, pinned storm 300 enemies q=high, 120 renders each, software raster (SwiftShader, so absolute ms are
  inflated; A/B only): Pit p95 82 / 76 ms · Ballroom p95 76 / 62 ms **with waltz rings + chandelier telegraph +
  mirror seam all active**. The ballroom skips gargoyles and red cracks, and everything new is baked: mirror tiles
  (≤48 sprites, own cache), the rosette (one 920px canvas), the chandelier and wreck sprites (one each). No per-frame
  gradients, no shadowBlur.
- Shots: `shots/d3-ballroom/` — f0 ante room card, f1 0:30, f2 telegraph, f3 the fall, f4 wreck + coins, f5 the waltz,
  f6 mirror twins, f7 5:00, f8 13:00 THE HOUSE. Script: `node probes/floorshots.js <label> --floor ballroom`.

## Files touched (merge notes)
- `src/ui.js` — FLOORS gets the `ballroom` entry; `curFloor()` honors `window.__probeFloor` (harness only); right after
  it, one self-contained block `/* ==== D3 — THE BALLROOM ==== */` (BAL state, BAL_SFX, music, balUpdate, balDrop,
  balImpact, balReflect, balTile, balMedallion, balGround, balSky + sprite bakers). State resets itself per run via
  `window._runSeq`, so `startGame` is untouched.
- `src/core.js` — 7 one-line hooks, all gated on `window._fmods.ballroom`:
  `update`: `balUpdate(dt)` just before the "waves" block. `render`: `const _bR=…` before the tile loop; tile blit picks
  `balTile`; `balMedallion` replaces `drawSigil`; gargoyles + floorCracks loops iterate `BAL_NONE` on the ballroom;
  `balGround()` after the stray candle pools (before `PROF.mark('ground')`); `balSky()` right after `drawPlayer()`.
- `src/ranks.js` — Index gets a **THE FLOORS** section (all floors, unlocked ones show their card text as tooltip, plus
  three Ballroom tiles: THE WALTZ, THE CHANDELIER, MIRROR TWINS). Not counted in `idxTotal` (index % unchanged).
- `probes/bot.js` — `--floor <id>` flag (sets `window.__probeFloor`), summary reports the floor.
- `probes/lib.js` — the bot steps out of `window.__hazards` circles (the ballroom exposes its telegraphs there).
- `probes/floorshots.js` — new floor screenshot script.
- No styles.css, no save keys, no new persistence (unlock reads REC.minutes / LT.houseMet).

## For the orchestrator
- **Stakes (B2):** the unlock calls `stakeCleared(1)` if a global function of that name exists. If B2 named it
  differently, change that one call in the FLOORS entry (ui.js).
- **Daily (B1):** the ballroom reads only `window._fmods`; its randomness is `Math.random`. If the daily forces a floor
  it just works; if the daily wants the ballroom seeded, route the `Math.random` calls in the D3 block through B1's PRNG.
- **Endless (B2):** after 13:00 the ballroom keeps dropping chandeliers at the House cadence and mirrors stay off while
  `houseActive`; if endless clears `houseActive`, mirrors resume — fine either way.
- If A2/others touched `render()` around the tile loop / `drawSigil` / `drawPlayer()` the hooks are trivially re-applied.
- Death by chandelier is credited in the ledger to whatever enemy stood nearest (hurtPlayer picks `_hitBy` itself);
  the announce `☠ CRUSHED UNDER THE CHANDELIER ☠` does fire.

## Known issues / not done
- The bot is not a human: ballroom median parity was reached with the bot dodging telegraphs; real players will
  probably find the floor *easier* than the Pit once they learn to lure the court under the glass.
- No dedicated music-channel hook (B3) — the ambience is synthesized directly via `tone()` and honors the AMBIENCE
  toggle; the waltz swell always plays (it is a gameplay signal).
