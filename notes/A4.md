# A4 — THE POCKET CASINO (phones + install as an app)

Branch `claude/a4-pocket-casino`. Built on origin/main @ 7dff2a6.

## What it feels like
Open it on a phone and the House fits in your hand. Put your left thumb down anywhere on the left
side of the floor and a **gilt ring** blooms under it with a warm gold knob. Push, and Vesper walks.
If your thumb overruns the ring, the ring follows it, so you never "fall off" the stick. Your right
thumb rests on a fat **gold BET coin** in the corner. It *is* the space bar: tap it to spin, stop,
snap chains and push the ascent. Hold it to roll the dice. On the ledger, hold it to re-ante. It
squashes when pressed, ticks with a felt click, and buzzes the phone very slightly.
A small **II** and a **✦ CODEX** chip sit under the coin counter. The ascent grows a mint **▲ CASH**
coin above BET while you're climbing. The pause card gets a **✦ YOUR CODEX** button. Its existing RESUME / HOW TO PLAY / QUIT pills
are lifted clear of the blood bar, and tapping the card itself still resumes.

The House also learns to speak touch: every "SPACE" it prints becomes "◈ BET", "WASD" becomes
"LEFT THUMB", ENTER keycaps become "▲" (the CASH coin's glyph), and "press SPACE" becomes "TAP".
The canvas hints (vault "◈ BET — ENTER", streak "◈ BET — CASH +n") change too.
On narrow screens the camera pulls back about 28%, so a portrait phone sees about 540×1170 world
pixels instead of 390×844. You can see the horde coming.

## What was built (by brief item)
1. **Controls** (`src/ui.js`, A4 block at the end of the file)
   - Floating stick: touch pointers on the canvas in the left 62% of the screen. The ring and knob are DOM
     elements (`#tcStick`) with pointer capture, a 16% deadzone, and the ring follows a thumb that
     overruns it. It feeds `touch.joy/jx/jy` into the movement code (one line in `core.js` update).
     Mouse hold-to-walk is unchanged.
   - BET coin `#tcBet` dispatches real `keydown`/`keyup` ' ' on window, so slot hold-to-roll, the
     dice, wheel, ascent, final press, rage cash, streak cash and vault entry all work through the
     existing keyboard code. Nothing was duplicated. The coin's label follows the state: BET / HOLD (dice
     offer) / LAUNCH·PUSH (ascent) / PRESS (final press) / READ·AGAIN (ledger).
   - On the ledger, BET is R: hold for 400ms or more and it re-antes. A shorter tap is SPACE, which
     finishes the recap and then deals again.
   - `#tcCash` = ENTER while the ascent is live. `#tcPause` = ESC. `#tcCodex` toggles the TAB codex
     (it freezes the game exactly like holding TAB; tap outside the columns to close).
   - The final press: on touch, any tap on the layer is also a press, so you can mash with both thumbs.
   - Swipe left/right turns pages in How to Play and the ledger page reveal.
   - Every other menu, parlor and panel already had click handlers. I audited jackpot cards, stake
     chips, Back/Close buttons, the vault, options and the how-to arrows.
2. **Layout** (`styles.css`, one block headed `/* ==== A4 ==== */` at the end; sizing and positioning only)
   - `viewport-fit=cover` and the safe-area insets are applied to the HUD top/bottom, mute and the thumb buttons.
   - The menu scrolls on small screens. Portrait uses a 2×3 grid of parlor buttons and a full-width PLAY.
     Landscape phones use two columns: title, chips, rank and PLAY on the left, parlors on the right; the chip row
     scrolls sideways.
   - Ledger, feats, index, story and vault scroll inside the safe area. The codex sheet stacks into one column.
   - The HUD's right column (level and relics) lifts above the BET coin. Coach and achievement toasts move away
     from the thumbs.
   - **Cabinet fitter** (`tcFit`, JS): it measures `#machine`, the wheel and ascent shells and the pause card,
     then shrinks them with the CSS `scale` property until they fit the glass. On 844×390 the slot runs at
     about 0.71. `scale` composes with any `transform` animation, so A2's machine shake still works.
   - Pull-to-refresh, double-tap zoom (`touch-action:manipulation`), long-press menus, text
     selection and iOS pinch are suppressed. The canvas already had `touch-action:none`.
   - Camera: `ZOOM` in `core.js resize()`. If the short side is under 540px, VW/VH become logical pixels
     (short side → 540, floor 0.72) and the canvas transform scales. This is visual only; no game numbers changed.
3. **Audio and perf**: initAudio + resume + a silent buffer play inside the first touchend, click or pointerup
   (iOS needs this; the existing touchstart kick stays). On touch devices `GFX.q==='auto'` starts on the low
   tier (DPR 1, light effects). Auto-quality can still climb if the device can hold it.
4. **PWA**: `manifest.webmanifest` (fullscreen, any orientation, velvet theme), `icons/` (a gilt ♠ on
   oxblood velvet, drawn as a path by `icons/make.js` in headless Chromium: 180, 192, 512 and maskable 512, plus SVG),
   and `sw.js`. The service worker is network-first with a cache fallback, so a deploy always arrives online and
   offline still plays. It precaches index.html, styles.css, src/*.js, ante-mortem.html and the icons; missing files
   are skipped. The manifest `<link>` and SW registration are injected **only on http(s)**, so
   `dist/ante-mortem.html` from file:// is untouched and has no console noise.
   The vite build also copies manifest (start_url → ante-mortem.html), sw.js and icons into `dist/` for a hosted
   dist. Those files are gitignored; only the single HTML is committed, as before.

## Verified (how)
- `npm run build`: clean.
- `npm run probe -- --nights 2 --mobile` exits 0 with 0 errors (390×844 touch) on four separate runs. Deaths ranged 2:42–8:07 at levels 3–6.
- `npm run probe -- --nights 3` (desktop) exits 0 with 0 errors. Desktop gameplay code is untouched; `ZOOM` is 1 at 1280×720 and the touch UI never
  switches on. Because the bot's death time varies a lot, I ran 5 nights on this branch and 5 on main's v4.0 build back to back:
  **A4 median 4:57** (3:59–6:09, levels 4–5) vs **main median 3:27** (2:58–7:35, levels 3–5). That is within the baseline band and not a regression.
- `npm run phone -- a4-after` (new, `probes/phone.js`) covers portrait 390×844, landscape 844×390 and tablet 768×1024 with touch
  emulation. Each pass: taps OPTIONS and PLAY, photographs the slot, **drives the slot with the BET coin only**, drags
  the stick (the player moved ~+85,−65 px in 20 frames each time), taps II, opens ✦ CODEX from the pause card, taps out and
  resumes, then dies to the ledger. There were 0 errors.
- `node probes/pwa.js` serves the repo root under `/ante-mortem/` the way GitHub Pages does. It checks the manifest and every icon, waits for the SW
  to activate (22 files cached), **goes offline, reloads, and starts a night**. `node probes/pwa.js --dist` runs the same check against dist/.
  Both are green.
- `node probes/phoneprof.js` is a real-clock frame profile at 390×844 on the touch tier. Render JS averages 2.05ms (p95 3.4ms) at
  1× CPU and 8.5ms (p95 15.3ms) at 4× CPU throttle, on the low tier. Headless Chromium rasterizes in software, so read the fps
  it reports as a floor, not a phone number.
- Screenshots: `shots/a4-after/` has the standard set (d1–d5, m1–m2) plus `p_{portrait,landscape,tablet}_{1menu,2options,3slot,4play,5pause,5codex,6ledger}.jpg`.

## Merge notes for the orchestrator
- **Files touched:** `src/ui.js` (canvas pointer handlers at ~L519 replaced; `touch` object gains joy fields;
  large A4 block appended at the end), `src/core.js` (4 small spots: `resize()` ZOOM, one movement line, two
  canvas hint strings, and a `typeof GFX` guard at the top of `frame()`), `styles.css` (A4 block at the end only),
  `index.html` (head metas + apple-touch-icon only), `vite.config.js` (emits the PWA files), `package.json`
  (`npm run phone`), `probes/lib.js` (two bot flags: `manual`, `pauseHold`), new `probes/phone.js`,
  `probes/pwa.js`, `probes/phoneprof.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icons/`.
- **Risky spots**
  - `core.js resize()`. If A1 adds offscreen light or bloom buffers sized from `VW*DPR`, they should size from
    `cv.width/cv.height` (device pixels) or multiply by `ZOOM`. VW/VH are now *logical* pixels (= screen / ZOOM).
    Anything mapping a screen pixel (clientX) to world must divide by `ZOOM`. Only `ptr()` in ui.js did that.
  - The `frame()` `typeof GFX` guard fixes a real hosted-build race: core.js's 15ms fallback ticker could run
    `frame()` before ui.js loaded and log `GFX is not defined` once. That also set `_amErr` and swallowed later
    error logs. The single-file build never hit it; GitHub Pages would.
  - If A2 restyles the slot or HUD: the fitter only reads `offsetWidth/Height` and writes `style.scale` on
    `#machine`, `.wheelShell` and `.pauseCard`. The touch CSS moves `.hudBottom>div:last-child` up 112px (96px in landscape).
    If A2 restructures `.hudBottom`, re-point that selector.
  - The label rewriter is a MutationObserver that runs only on touch devices and rewrites text nodes containing
    SPACE, WASD or ENTER. New UI text written with "SPACE" gets translated automatically.
  - If B3 (gamepad/rebinding) changes the keydown handler: BET depends on `key:' '` keydown/keyup, CASH on
    `'Enter'`, II on `'Escape'`, and the ledger hold on `'r'`.
  - Bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` whenever you want installed phones to drop old caches. It's network-first, so they
    get new code anyway when online.

- Not mine, spotted in passing: on a fresh save, OPTIONS shows **COLORBLIND PIPS: UNDEFINED** and **TAB CODEX: UNDEFINED**.
  Those GFX keys have no default, so the label prints `undefined`. It's pre-existing on main. A2 or B3 should give them defaults.
- The pre-existing one-seat guard (`am_seat_v1`, 4s heartbeat) shows "this table is already dealt in another tab"
  if a phone reloads the app within 4s. TAKE THE SEAT clears it. I left it alone because it's not my lane.
- The HUD stays visible behind the death ledger (pre-existing on desktop too). On a landscape phone the ledger's
  first screen is mostly the HUD and the title; it scrolls.
- Achievement toasts are transient but can still sit over part of a panel on a small phone.
