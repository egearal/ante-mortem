# B2 — THE STAKES + THE ENDLESS FLOOR

Branch `claude/b2-stakes-endless`, rebased onto origin/main after Wave A (A1+A2+A3+A4) landed.
The force-push for the rebase was refused by the sandbox, so the branch tip carries a `-s ours` merge of
its own pre-rebase history. **The tree is the rebased one.** `git diff origin/main` shows only B2's work.
Merging it, or squashing it, is clean.

## What it feels like
**You beat the night.** Stand alive at 15:00, when the House's reign ends, and the floor no longer
forecloses you. Instead the screen slams **THE NIGHT IS WON**: a mega jackpot, confetti, a seraph choir,
and the House concedes ("...FINE. THE NIGHT IS YOURS. STAY, IF YOU DARE."). Then a velvet card offers you
a choice. **∞ PRESS ON** (SPACE / the BET coin) or **◈ CASH OUT** (X).

**THE STAKES.** The first win unseals a deck ladder in the Ante Room. Each deck is a little card-back
badge in its own color, and each one keeps every cruelty below it:
- ♦ **CRIMSON**: *Sharpshooters.* Shooters and hexers fire where you're GOING, so straight-line kiting
  gets punished and zig-zags live. ×1.25 marks & gilt. First clear: ✹ BLOOD HALO + 500 gilt.
- ♣ **OBSIDIAN**: *The Paired Court.* Every elite walks in with a twin. ×1.5. First clear: a new soul,
  ♝ **THE OBSIDIAN TWIN**, Vesper's shadow (+1 projectile, dice −1, −30 blood) + 1,000 gilt. D1's seat
  ceremony celebrates it too.
- ♥ **IVORY**: *Marked Cards.* Every dice target is +1, so the House reads your hand. ×1.75.
  First clear: ♛ IVORY CROWN + 2,000.
- ☠ **BONE**: *Long Curses.* Every timed curse lasts twice as long. ×2.
  First clear: ☠ BONE CHOIR (two frost skulls orbit you) + 3,500.
- ♛ **GILT**: *The Early Landlord.* The House arrives at **12:00**, so you face three whole minutes of
  its reign. ×2.5. First clear: ✵ GILT MANTLE (gold motes rise off your coat) + 7,500.

Adornments are pure cosmetics. You wear one at a time from the Ante Room's ADORNMENTS row. Stake 1 opens
for every soul after the first win. Each deeper deck must be cleared **with that soul**. That makes six
souls × five decks of long-term chase. The menu's loadout line shows the chosen deck and that soul's ◆ pips.

**THE ENDLESS FLOOR.** PRESS ON and the House steps back. The clock keeps running past 15:00, the timer
glows violet, and the vignette sinks deeper into blood-violet every minute. Each wave the court deals a
theme: THE PROCESSION (wraiths/stalkers), THE GALLERY (shooters/hexers), THE OSSUARY (brutes/revenants),
THE TIDE (swarm/husks/bombers) or THE LEECHING. Elite waves come every 2nd wave, and the elites grow
fatter each minute. A **champion** from the boss roster arrives every 3:00 (first at +1:30) and is heavier
each time. A HUD plaque under the coins reads **∞ ENDLESS +m:ss ×n.n**: the score multiplier climbs ×0.1
every 30s, and each step slams with a rising ding. Every minute "THE FLOOR SINKS". Death ends the night
as normal. The ledger shows the deck row (per-soul ◆ pips) and the ENDLESS row (time, ×, and
✦ DEEPEST EVER ✦ on a record). The share line gains `· ♦ crimson deck ◆ · endless +2:14`.

## Verified (all on the BUILT single file, after the rebase onto Wave A)
- `npm run build`: clean (13 scripts parse).
- `npm run probe -- --nights 3`: exit 0, **0 errors**, median 3:33.
- **Unstaked balance A/B.** Probe death times are very noisy:
  - main before Wave A, 21 nights: median 4:29, range 2:20–13:06
  - B2, 16 nights: median 4:25
  - B2 with its per-frame hooks stubbed out, 8 nights: the same spread

  Stake 0 runs exactly the old code paths (every modifier is flag-gated). The v4.0 "4:50–6:40" figure sits
  inside this noise, and A3's fresh-save tuning had already moved it.
- **Every stake to completion** (`npm run probe -- --nights 1 --stake N --god-until 930 --minutes 18`):
  - All five decks won at 15:00, pressed on, then died on the endless floor at +31–36s once god turned off. 0 errors each.
  - Re-run after the rebase: stake 5 on desktop and stake 2 on a **phone viewport** (`--mobile`) are both clean.
  - Re-run after merging D1: stake 2 seated as `--soul twin`, and stake 5 as `--soul martyr` on a phone. Both won, 0 errors.
  - The merged build's `npm run probe -- --nights 3` gave 0 errors (median 9:43, noise).
- **Perf** (`node probes/b2perf.js`): the endless court is dealt through its real startWave path to 300
  walkers plus a champion, then compared with a plain-night 300-storm measured the same way:
  - Endless **CPU p95 6.0–7.3ms** across four runs (latest, merged with D1: 7.1ms), against 7.0–8.1ms for
    the baseline (budget 12ms).
  - Caps hold: ≤300 walkers, ≤8 elites, one champion at a time.
  - **Measurement note:** this container rasterizes on a software GPU. Every ~6 frames a ~400ms raster flush
    lands *whatever is on screen*: an EMPTY floor shows it too. So whole-frame p95 means nothing here, and
    the gate uses the non-flush frames (equivalent to the old hidden-tab protocol). Absolute numbers
    deserve a check on real hardware (B4).

  If the director wants them sooner, relax `b2Avail` / `b2NightWon`'s trigger (e.g. also unlock after
  meeting the House N times).
- `#bossBar` stays drawn over the death ledger if a boss is alive at death. This is pre-existing HUD
  layering, visible in `b6_endless_ledger.jpg`. Not fixed (not my lane).
- The god bot earns `DEATHLESS` in probes because it is never touched. That's the harness, not the game.
