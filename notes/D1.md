# D1 — THE SEATS (new playable souls + portraits)

Branch `claude/d1-seats`. Four new souls, a painted portrait for all ten, an unlock ceremony, an Index section.

## What it feels like now
**The Ante Room has faces.** Every soul card carries an oval cameo in a gilt frame: a candlelit bust on velvet black,
rim-lit in the soul's own color, eyes glowing. Vesper in her crimson coat and feathered brim; the Dealer under a
blue glass eyeshade with a fan of cards; the Widow behind a violet veil with a moth at her throat; the Debtor in a
dented top hat, monocle and padlocked chain; the Brute bald, scarred, iron-collared, spiked pauldrons; the Yolk peeking
out of its cracked shell. Sealed seats show the same bust as a **black silhouette with only the eyes burning**, the
name, the condition, and a progress bar ("4 / 10") — you can see who you're working toward.

**The four new souls:**
- **♣ THE CARDSHARP** (emerald) — *LAND 3 TRIPLES.* Every slot opens with THE TELL: the card shows exactly what the
  pull will give before you spin — "IRON CONSTITUTION · ♣ THE TELL: PAIR — +2 LEVELS". Don't like it? **R** (or the green
  RE-DEAL button) burns 25 coins and deals a fresh hand, once per slot — riffle sound, green flash, card flips in.
  The reels then land exactly as told (loose hands become a fated single the selector lands on). −15 max blood.
- **✝ THE MARTYR** (crimson) — *SIT AT THE RED TABLE 10 TIMES.* Nothing heals her: regen, leech, fountains, wheel
  heals, tarot heals all get "✝ REFUSED". Every 10% of blood lost tolls a bell and stamps STIGMATA I…IX:
  +6% damage and +4% fire rate per step (up to +54% / +36%), and a crimson halo thickens around her. +40 max blood.
  Resurrection isn't healing — the Death Roll, Phoenix and Second Chance still work.
- **✧ THE MAGPIE** (cyan) — *PICK UP 5,000 LIFETIME COINS.* Pickup reach ×2.5, −20% damage. 3% of coins drop as a
  red-and-gold casino chip with a cyan glow; grab one and a little three-reel slot pops up under the banner and spins
  without stopping the night: 7-7-7 (12%) = a free level, a pair (35%) = a free mini-power, a bust = 8–15 coins.
- **♖ THE BANKER'S GHOST** (chartreuse) — *CASH OUT RAGE AT 90%+ 15 TIMES* (counted for every soul).
  Every 60s: "♖ INTEREST ACCRUES — +N COINS" (8% of the purse, ka-ching + coin rain, a 5-second warning first).
  Every 90%+ RAGE cash-out also pays a dividend of 16% of the purse. The Wheel of Souls never manifests for him. −10 max blood.

**Unlocking** any soul (old or new) fires the ceremony once: a framed toast with the portrait sliding in
("✦ A NEW SOUL TAKES A SEAT ✦"), a low bell, a rising six-note arpeggio, chip stack, confetti, coin rain, a flash
in the soul's color. It fires on the menu, the ledger, or mid-run (checked every 4s). **The death ledger** now opens its
run record with the portrait of whoever sat tonight. **The Index** has a THE SEATS section (hover for the full card text).

## Known issues
- Estate % for veterans dips slightly (souls are 1/20 of the estate and the denominator grew from 6 to 10).
- Martyr's damage/fire bonus is applied multiplicatively to P.damage/P.fireRate; additive debuff reverts from curses
  can shift it by a hair. RAGE snapshots are rescaled so RAGE never eats or doubles the stigmata.
- The Cardsharp tell can't foresee the 1% SERAPH upgrade (it's a surprise on top — the House still cheats).
- The Magpie chip's `pending.push({})` opens a level slot right away, as a level-up would.
- Final baseline `npm run probe -- --nights 3` (Vesper, built file): 4:51 L6 / 4:54 L6 / 4:50 L5 — median **4:51**, 0 errors (baseline band 4:50–6:40).
