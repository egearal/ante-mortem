## What changed

### 1. HOW TO PLAY learned tonight's systems
Five new pages after THE LONG GAME, in the House's voice. Each one has three short entries so it fits
without scrolling at 1280×720 (300px of a 403px body) and 390×844 (≤383 of ~397px). The tabs shrink on
phones so all ten wrap into four rows.
- **♛ FATES**: Pairings (how to evolve), Major Arcana (read the name before you step on), ∞ PRESS ON.
- **⚔ HIGH ROLLERS**: the Croupier's cards + coin flip, the Bookie's odds markers, the Twins' roulette (hit ×1).
- **☀ NEW TABLES**: Daily Debt (first try scored, keep the streak), the five Stakes, the Book of Debts.
- **♕ NEW FACES**: the Ballroom (waltz, chandeliers, mirror twins) and the four new souls in one line each.
- **⚙ HANDS & DIALS**: pad map, phone thumbs, where SETTINGS lives.
The B3/A4 label rewriters still turn SPACE into Ⓐ / ◈ BET on these pages, because they run on the same text.

### 2. The TAB codex shows pairing recipes
A new **♛ PAIRINGS** strip under SKILLS lists one row per weapon you carry: `♠ MAX Bone Bolt + ❖ Extra Ante →
💀 THE OSSUARY · Bone Bolt 6/6 · skill ✓`. Each row's status turns into **✓ READY — NEXT LEVEL** or **♛ EVOLVED**.
You never have to guess a recipe again. Text comes straight from `PAIRINGS` / `PASSIVES`, so it can't drift.

### 3. QA fixes
- **The boss bar sat on the death ledger.** When THE HOUSE (or any boss) killed you, its purple bar stayed on top of
  "A RECORD IN BLOOD" (it's visible in `shots/night-final/d5_death.jpg`). `marker.js` now hides `#bossBar` when the
  ledger opens, and the HUD re-shows it the next time a boss lives.
- **Fuzz false alarm.** `probes/fuzz.js` failed "DOM grew 817 nodes night 2→5". I traced it per container: the Wheel of
  Souls builds its ~830-node machine **once**, the first night it manifests, and keeps it. On a run where it first
  appeared on night 5, the check fired. When the wheel shows up on night 1 the count is flat (1800→1893). The probe
  now excludes `#wheelLayer` from the count. After the fix: CLEAN, DOM 946/1013/969/991/998.
