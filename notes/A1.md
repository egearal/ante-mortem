# A1 — GILT NOIR: THE FLOOR AND THE COURT

Branch `claude/a1-gilt-noir-world`. World rendering only. Zero gameplay numbers changed.

## What it feels like now
The hall went dark and expensive. The marble is velvet-black with only a ghost of veining; the gold
sigil and the crimson runner are engraved into the dark instead of painted over it. Light is a thing
you carry: Vesper walks inside her own warm candle-pool, two more candle pools wander the floor, and
the screen edges fall away into black. Against that, the court finally *reads*: every enemy is cut
out of the floor by an ink outline, its edge catches her candlelight on the side facing her, and its
eyes glow and drift toward her. Elites wear a breathing violet aura in their own shape; bosses a red one.

Vesper is 1.45× bigger on screen (hitbox untouched): long crimson coat with an ink outline and a
white-gold rim where her own light wraps her shoulders, wide brim hat with a gold band and a red
feather, a pale face with ember eyes that follow the aim, a 4-beat run (boots plant and lift, coat
tails swing, free arm pumps), and an idle breathe when she stands still. She is the brightest thing
on screen.

Hits land. Every struck enemy flashes pure white **in its own silhouette** (not a blob). Kills pop
with a white-hot burst that grows by tier — trash gets a spark, heavies (r≥18 or ≥90 HP) a ring and a
low thump, elites a 40ms hit-stop + crack + sub-boom, bosses 90ms. When Vesper is struck she flashes
white, blood leaps off her, the screen edges bleed red for a beat and a sub-thump lands under the
existing hurt sound. Screen shake decays faster the harder it is — short and sharp.

Pickups: XP souls are now **mint diamonds** (they bloom mint), coins are **spinning gold discs**
(they bloom gold). Different shape AND color; gold is only on what pays. Skeletal archers went
bone-ash and the Croupier ivory, so no enemy wears gold except the Gilded (which pays 14 coins).

## Profiler (pinned storm: 300 enemies + 1 elite, 1280×720, seeded RNG, q pinned)
Measured with a scratch harness (virtual clock for setup, real clock + `__prof` stages during 200
`render()` calls, `getImageData` after each to force raster). Hidden-tab SwiftShader, so absolute ms are
software raster; within-run A/B only.
| | origin/main | A1 |
|---|---|---|
| q=high, CPU stage sum (`__prof`) | 13.4 / 14.1 ms | 13.9 / 12.9 ms |
| q=high, enemies stage | 3.0 / 3.1 ms | 3.5 / 3.7 ms |
| q=high, full raster avg | 108–113 ms | 112–121 ms |
| q=low, CPU stage sum | 13.0 / 12.9 ms | 12.2 / 13.3 ms |
| q=low, full raster avg | 108–114 ms | 110–118 ms |
CPU submit cost is at parity (fog halved paid for the ink plates/eyes/light). Software raster is
within noise to ~+5%; on a GPU additive blits are near free. Discipline kept: no new per-frame
shadowBlur (removed two old ones on Vesper's eyes/card), no per-frame gradient creation (all new
gradients are cached via `_lgCache` or baked), every new silhouette/light is a baked canvas.
