# C2 — THE HIGH ROLLERS

Branch `claude/c2-high-rollers`. New gambling bosses, a new boss rotation, two elite affixes, boss sounds.

## What it feels like now
The lords of the House stopped being big men who shoot rings. Now each fight is a table game you play with your life.

**THE CROUPIER** (rebuilt, same id so Index and achievements carry over). An ivory dealer in a black waistcoat and
green visor, red bow tie, a fan of cards in his outstretched hand. Every ~6s he snaps his wrist and **deals five cards
face-down onto the floor in a fan** toward you. One by one they flip: **♠** a lane lights up steel-white and three blades
scream down it; **♥** a red dashed tether runs back to him and he heals ~4% unless you step on the heart and *palm it*
(+8 HP for you); **♦** the card bursts into 6 coins (run in and take them); **♣** an icy ring that slows you 45% for 5s.
The boss bar shows the deck's honest odds: `♠40 ♥20 ♦15 ♣25`. At 60% and 25% HP he **flips a coin over your head**:
you hold ♛ HEADS or ☠ TAILS (random), the coin spins faster and faster, and you hit **SPACE / ◈ BET when your face is
showing**. Catch it flat and it's your face; catch it on the edge and it's 50/50 (the text under the coin says exactly
that). Win → "CALLED IT", he staggers **EXPOSED ×1.5 damage for 4s**. Lose → he's **ENRAGED for 6s** (×1.45 speed and deal rate).

**THE BOOKIE** (new). A squat man in racetrack-green check with a bowler, carrying a **slate ledger on his back that
IS his life bar**: "OWED ◈ 5,569" in chalk plus ten tallies that go red as he bleeds; the boss bar reads
`THE BOOKIE — LEDGER ◈ 5,569 OWED`. Every ~13s he **opens the board**: three glowing odds markers appear —
**2:1** (55% · hits 6%) safely behind you, **5:1** (35% · hits 14%) beside him, **10:1** (22% · hits 28%) *right under
him*, while he parks on it and sprays chalk rings. "BETS CLOSE IN 3… 2… 1…", a race bell — if you're standing in a
marker it pays: win = a chunk ripped out of his ledger, a red PAID stamp, coins, and a bigWin for 5:1 / 10:1; lose = a
real curse from the curse pool. Not standing anywhere: "NO BET — THE BOOKIE SHRUGS". Between boards he throws betting
slips (3-shot fans).

**THE TWINS OF FORTUNE** (new). Two slim jester-hooded figures, one antique gold with a smiling mask, one bone with a
weeping mask, chained together by a crackling line (touching the chain hurts). They orbit you; the gold one telegraphs
and dashes through, the bone one throws 5-shard fans. **One shared life.** A small **roulette wheel of gold and bone
pockets hangs over them**; every 8s it ticks, spins ("NO MORE BETS") and lands — only the twin matching the wheel takes
full damage (it glows and wears `×1`); the other takes 40% (grey dashed ward, `×0.4`). The bar says which one bleeds.

**Unique bounties** (on top of the usual gilt + epic floor): Croupier → **THE DEALER'S CUT +6% crit for the night**;
Bookie → **PAID IN FULL: every active curse torn up + 40 coins**; Twins → **FORTUNE'S FAVOR +10% damage for the night**.

**Entrances**: nameplate now carries a rules line under the name (e.g. "STAND ON THE ODDS WHEN BETS CLOSE · WIN — HE
BLEEDS · LOSE — A CURSE"), then a typed voice line in the boss's color with a synthesized mutter, over a music sting:
the Croupier's card riffle + a minor tango phrase, the Bookie's ticker tape + brass stab + "they're off" bell, the
Twins' two-voice music box resolving on a tritone.

**Elite affixes** (24% LOADED, 24% MARKED, rest plain), with a colored plate under the elite:
- **LOADED** (orchid `#ff8af7`, a die orbits it): every ~4.6s it throws a die that tumbles and lands on 1–6; every
  non-boss enemy within 170px is re-rolled to ×0.55 / 0.7 / 0.85 / 1.15 / 1.3 / 1.5 speed for 6s (green ring + "SLOWED"
  if it rolled low, red "HASTED" if high).
- **MARKED** (orange `#ff5a1f`, a rotating reticle): the first crit that hits it splits it into two smaller elites
  (half HP each, half coins/xp each), tagged "MARKED · SPLIT". Splits never split again.

## Boss rotation (core.js `hrBossOrder`, per night)
Slot 1 (wave 10) ∈ {Pit Boss, Croupier, Bookie} · slot 2 (wave 20) ∈ {Croupier, Bookie, Collector} minus slot 1 ·
slot 3 (wave 30) = Twins · slot 4 (wave 40) = The House Itself. Lords the player has never met are preferred (70%).
Base HP: Croupier 2100 (unchanged), Bookie 2400, Twins 2000 shared (time-scaled like every boss).
`spawnBoss(kind)` now accepts a forced kind (used by the probe; nothing in the game passes one).

### Frame budget — honest note
This sandbox's headless Chromium stalls ~6–12% of frames for 300–900 ms **even on an empty floor with no boss**
(measured with the game's own `__prof` on the real clock: no boss p50 1.8 ms but p95 836 ms). So a raw p95 is
meaningless here. For the frames that aren't environment stalls, update+render CPU during each fight is:
**Croupier p50 2.9 / p85 5.4 ms, Bookie 1.6 / 2.7 ms, Twins 1.4 / 2.4 ms** — the Pit Boss in the same harness was
2.6 / 5.0 ms. The new bosses cost the same as the old ones. All new bodies are baked once per kind (`hrBake`, 160px,
ink outline + gradient + rim), the silhouettes reuse A1's `_silShape` (so ink plate, boss glow, white hit flash and
afterimages all match), and there is **no shadowBlur** and no per-frame gradient creation in any C2 draw code.
Please re-check p95 on a real GPU machine.
