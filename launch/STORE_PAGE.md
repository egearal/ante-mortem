**WHAT THE HOUSE DEALS**

- ♠ **Survivors combat with a gambler's heart** — auto-fire, hordes, and a slot machine every level-up, with visible odds, near-misses and fanfares that escalate with the stakes.
- ⚀ **Press your luck everywhere** — the dice ladder, the Wheel of Souls, a crash-game **Ascent**, and the **Death Roll** when you fall: double or nothing on your own corpse.
- ⚔ **29 weapons, 10 secret Pairings** — max a weapon while holding its partner skill and it *evolves*, with a ceremony the size of a jackpot.
- ✦ **12 Major Arcana** — rare tarot cards that bend the whole night: the Tower brings the ceiling down, Death lets you skip your own.
- ♛ **The High Rollers** — the Croupier deals hazard cards and calls a coin, the Bookie makes you stand on the odds, the Twins of Fortune swap who can bleed with every spin. And at 13:00, THE HOUSE.
- 🎭 **10 souls, 4 floors** — the Cardsharp sees the reels before you pull, the Martyr grows stronger as she bleeds, the Banker's Ghost earns interest. Descend from the Pit to the Cellar, the Chapel and the Ballroom, where the chandeliers fall.
- 📅 **Daily Debt** — one seeded night per day, the same for every player in the world. Streaks, a share card, bragging rights.
- 📖 **The Book of Debts** — every unlock in one ledger, your three nearest goals pinned. 100 House ranks, 5 Stakes, an Endless floor past 13:00.
- 📱 **Plays anywhere** — one small file, no install. Thumb stick on phones, full controller and Steam Deck support, rebindable keys, reduce-flashing.

> **FAKE CURRENCY ONLY.** Every coin, chip and gilt is fake. Nothing can be bought, sold, cashed out or won outside the game. The House pays in nothing you can spend.
